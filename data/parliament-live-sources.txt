FTN PARLIAMENT CHANNEL — OFFICIAL SOURCE REGISTRY
Version: 0.1 (foundation)
Purpose: provide a source-controlled directory for official parliamentary broadcasts,
orders of business, chamber records and archives. Caribbean sources always appear first.

NON-NEGOTIABLE SOURCE RULES
- Link only to a parliament/legislature's official website or official, verified broadcaster account.
- "LIVE" means the source exposes a live/current stream or a published sittings schedule.
  It does not mean that a sitting is occurring at this moment.
- Do not rehost a legislature's stream without explicit permission.
- Preserve source URL, source type, verification status and last checked date per record.
- An editor must verify any source before marking it "verified-live".
