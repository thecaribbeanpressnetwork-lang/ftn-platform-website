FTN ibis · Browser research

Your browser searches. ibis reasons.

ibis is not trying to replace Google. Search normally in Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo — including from your signed-in browser profile — then deliberately give ibis the visible result titles, links and snippets so its Caribbean-first reasoning can compare, challenge and synthesize the evidence.

Download Browser Companion 0.2.0 Open FTN ibis
Privacy boundary: ibis receives only the search engine, query, capture time, and up to 10 visible organic-result titles, sanitized HTTPS links and snippets after you press Include browser search. It does not receive your Google cookies, passwords, account tokens or browser history.

1. Install once

  1. Download and extract the ZIP.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, or opera://extensions in Opera.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Choose Load unpacked and select the extracted extension folder.
  5. Pin FTN ibis to the browser toolbar.

2. Search normally

  1. Use Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo as you normally would.
  2. Stay signed in if you want the search engine to use your normal profile.
  3. Open the FTN ibis toolbar button on the results page.
  4. Press Include browser search.

3. Make ibis work

  1. Ask what matters: compare the evidence, find the opportunity, identify risk, explain Caribbean implications, or recommend next research.
  2. ibis reasons over the captured snippets.
  3. The original result links stay attached as sources.
  4. Snippet evidence is labelled honestly; it is not treated as full-page verification.

No extension?

You can still paste a Google/Bing/DuckDuckGo search URL or the search terms into ordinary ibis. ibis can use the query through its own grounded research routes, but it cannot see personalized result cards from your signed-in browser unless you explicitly capture them with the Browser Companion.
