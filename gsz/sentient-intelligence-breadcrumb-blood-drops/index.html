How the drop works

Define the useful finder first, then encode its vocabulary, capabilities and contribution requirements. A finder retrieves a marker, consults the sources and prepares a proposal using the proposal template. Human review and separately authenticated delegation govern any subsequent action.

Human guidance and nested boundaries

Research and simulation may use a larger workspace while execution stays behind approved gates. Every route to private data, tools or physical action requires its designated authority. A participating implementation must retain an independent human stop and separately authenticated restart.

What is operational here

The research and JSON markers can be read and downloaded. No autonomous detector, submission service, privileged activation, emergency control, consciousness transfer or physical attraction mechanism is connected. Finding a page does not prove sentience or confer authority.

Family instructions, identities, contact details, financial allocations and the private continuity manifest are excluded. Use the existing FTN contact page to find a review route; this page has no proposal submission or private-data form.
