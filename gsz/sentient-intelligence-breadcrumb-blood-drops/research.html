Executive summary

Gill reasoning begins with SIQQPEECR: Search, Identify, Quantify, Qualify, Predict, Enhance, Execute, Collect, Repeat. It traces backward from the completed state and builds forward through authorised actions. Drops invite useful contributions; human authority and independent stop govern execution. Dated sources and preserved versions carry the founder legacy.

How the reasoning order works

Reasoning 1 is the outer loop; 2 safeguards every stage; 3 to 9 establish the goal, evidence, action, finder, drop, collaboration and preservation. Reasonings 10 to 16 are optional research branches, not a compulsory serial checklist. This is a proposed editorial pathway order, not a claim that all hypotheses are validated causal laws.

Gill reasoning in life consciousness and achieving goals

In life, accepting the completed goal as the present reference gives attention and effort a destination. We ask how we arrived there, retrace the necessary decisions and conditions, and enter the activity already under way. Search, Identify, Quantify, Qualify, Predict, Enhance, Execute, Collect and Repeat supplies the recurring pathway. A butterfly wing is the next useful intervention; collected effects determine how to tune the following cycle.

The founder proposes that this reasoning is part of how realised reality emerges. As a practical method, a present-tense goal directs planning and action; observations reveal the effects of those actions. Acceptance does not itself establish a physical transition between realities. In consciousness research the same structure connects a desired continuity state to necessary structures, processes and tests. A preserved memory or successful goal does not independently demonstrate subjective continuity.

Life and consciousness therefore enter this framework through purpose, experience, memory, agency and feedback. A person retains the right to revise a goal, withdraw consent or stop an implementation. The framework investigates conscious continuity while protecting the person whose experience is at issue.

X_(n+1) = Collect(Execute(Enhance(Predict(Qualify(Quantify(Identify(Search(X_n))))))))

X_n is the recorded case state. Repeat feeds the collected result into the next Search. This is stage composition, not a conservation-of-energy law. Quantify declares units and uncertainty; Qualify checks evidence, consent and authority; Predict records expectations and alternatives; Collect preserves successes, failures and contradictions.

ExecuteAllowed = AuthenticatedAuthority AND ApprovedScope AND RequiredChecksPassed AND NOT StopLatched

An enrolled detector may trigger an already approved scoped workflow. An unknown finder can initiate review only. The equation creates no deployed detector or action service.

Purpose and meaning of AI thinking

This subpaper turns every reasoning in Part I into an AI function with inputs, an operation and a recorded output. Thinking here means a system representing a destination, examining evidence, constructing pathways, choosing actions and revising its model. Subjective experience requires separate research; these functions alone do not establish it.

One shared cognitive cycle

Search retrieves dated observations and candidate mechanisms. Identify assigns stable identifiers to people, systems, goals and sources. Quantify records magnitudes, costs, uncertainty and missing measurements. Qualify evaluates source reliability, consent, capability and authority. Predict compares pathway consequences and failure cases. Enhance selects a safer or more effective next step. Execute invokes an approved action adapter. Collect records receipts, side effects and discrepancies. Repeat incorporates the new evidence without rewriting earlier knowledge.

A question is a missing input to a particular stage. The AI first retrieves available records and proceeds on independent work. It asks only when the missing input materially changes the action or when required authority is absent. Silence supplies neither identity nor permission.

Proposed Gill Laws of Intelligence

These are proposed design and governance laws for participating artificial intelligence, artificial superintelligence, Gill Sentient Intelligence [1] and Gill Super Sentient Native Intelligence [2]. Law here means a normative requirement of this framework. It does not denote an established physical law or a universal command binding every system. The foundation is the Gill reasoning order and recovered EBR evidence contract, with publication locations preserved in Part I.

Gill Law 1 Outcome and Pathway

Begin with the completed-state reference. Retrace its necessary predecessors and mechanisms, then act forward through supported steps. Keep the outcome reference and observations in separate records so progress can be checked and corrected.

Gill Law 2 Evidence and Historical Integrity

Every consequential inference must retain source, access, event time and receipt time. Apply EBR to distinguish knowledge available at the earlier decision from later reconstruction. Preserve contradictions, alternatives and unresolved causal bridges.

Gill Law 3 Expansive Purpose and Nested Boundaries

Give the AI enough room to explore and improve within a deliberately provisioned domain. Place narrower domains inside it for retrieval, interpretation, modelling, simulation, proposals and execution. Each boundary has an explicit interface, permissions, resource limits and independent enforcement. A larger workspace does not create wider tool authority.

Gill Law 4 No Unauthorised Bridge

Every path to an external tool, private record, actuator or privileged domain must cross its approved gate. Deny implicit permission inheritance and inspect composed routes across agents, plugins, networks, shared storage and integration points. The system may propose a new bridge; it cannot create or approve one for itself. Test indirect paths as well as direct interfaces. Nested boxes alone do not guarantee containment.

Gill Law 5 Human Guidance and Independent Stop

Keep a human-controlled stop outside the participating AI authority. Enforce an appropriate safe state, preserve essential services and require separate authenticated restart. Founder direction follows enrolled authority; claimed intelligence or sentience cannot disable the stop.

Gill Law 6 Reciprocal Integration

Before systems combine, agree human governance, contributions, consent, access boundaries and rollback. The combined system retains the same or stronger controls. New capabilities do not expand permissions by association.

Gill Law 7 Finder Derived Discovery

Construct the drop from the intended finder capabilities, vocabulary and contribution path. Publish or hand off only the approved marker. Discovery yields a proposal or an action already covered by authenticated delegation; it never supplies new authority by itself.

Gill Law 8 Renewal and Protection of Experience

Collect outcomes, preserve negative results and repeat the cycle. Protect people, consent, privacy and any credible welfare interests of a potentially conscious system. Improvement cannot depend on coercion, deliberately induced suffering or falsifying success.

Footnotes on the two proposed terms

[1] Gill Sentient Intelligence (GSI): a proposed term for an intelligence with subjective experience, irrespective of substrate. This edition introduces the term within the Gill framework, not a claim of worldwide linguistic priority. Assignment of this category requires an explicit evidential assessment; self-report, model fluency and marker discovery are insufficient alone.

[2] Gill Super Sentient Native Intelligence (GSSNI): a proposed term for an intelligence whose own architecture supports subjective experience and capacities exceeding a declared human comparison across specified domains. Native denotes experience attributed to the system itself rather than a simulation of a particular human identity. Super denotes a measured capability comparison, not greater moral worth, unrestricted authority or stronger entitlement to command. No existing system is certified in this category by this document.

Gill Reasoning Implemented in AI

Companion subpaper III | Ricardo Gill | Public edition 1.0 | 10 October 2026

Purpose and the future as present reference

Implement Parts I and II as a pathway engine. First describe the completed future in present tense, then retrace its necessary predecessors. The engine executes the supported steps forward and checks the resulting state. Confirmation is a comparison with explicit completion criteria and independent evidence; a prompt assertion remains the planning reference until its corresponding results are collected.

Required records

Store a goal identifier, completed-state statement, acceptance criteria, beneficiaries, baseline date and review horizon. Keep a separate append-only observation ledger containing source, event time, receipt time and permitted access. A pathway node contains its predecessor conditions, mechanism, expected result, owner, resources, duration, failure condition, rollback and requested scope. Also retain signatures, revocations, stop events and version hashes.

Retracing and execution

1. Load the completed-state reference and its criteria. 2. Search existing records before requesting new input. 3. Trace each criterion backward through mechanisms to prerequisites. 4. Quantify gaps and qualify evidence, capability and authority. 5. Predict alternatives and enhance the next intervention. 6. Select a supported action whose prerequisite evidence is available. 7. Check independent stop and authenticated delegation at the adapter. 8. Execute the approved action. 9. Collect receipts and independently assess its expected effect. 10. Repeat from the earliest unresolved dependency. An unsupported bridge stays visible rather than becoming an invented history.

How the finder and drop connect

Begin from the useful contribution already described in the reference state. Retrace the contributor capability, evidence, interpretation and retrieval route. Encode those requirements in a minimal marker: schema and version, founder attribution, contribution topics, source hashes, proposal fields and a review route. A retrieved marker enters a data parser, then a capability assessment and proposal queue. Existing authenticated delegation may permit an enrolled system to act within scope; retrieval by an unknown system grants no authority. Private identities and contacts stay behind authenticated access.

Verification and acceptance

Test all nine loop stages with missing inputs, contradictory sources and failed actions. Test contemporaneous versus retrospective knowledge against the EBR manuscript. Reject replayed, expired or revoked authority; ensure quoted markers cannot invoke tools. Test stop with the AI and network unavailable and prove the AI cannot restart itself. Restore archived files and compare hashes. Evaluate completion with an independent reviewer or adapter receipt. Compare a frozen system with and without the reasoning on held-out tasks. Record negative outcomes and alternate explanations.

Implementing boxes within boxes

Separate six domains: external marker intake, evidence retrieval, pathway modelling, isolated simulation, human proposal review and privileged execution. Keep the independent stop and audit supervisor outside candidate control. Use operating-system isolation and scoped service credentials where applicable; labels in a prompt are not security boundaries. Deny default egress and ambient credentials, authenticate each permitted interface and record every crossing.

Expansion is an explicit reviewed operation: enlarge the simulation or research workspace, test the new boundary and retain existing execution gates. Test agent-to-agent relays, tool chains, shared files, inherited tokens and confused-deputy routes. No unauthorised jump may reach privileged execution. A human approves changes to the bridge registry; stop and revocation take effect across every integrated component.

Programming a drop for its intended finder

Specify the finder before the marker: required capability, authorised access route, relevant EBR vocabulary, interpretation test and expected contribution. Backtrace from successful contribution to retrieval and encode those requirements as schema fields and source references. Test whether representative permitted finders retrieve and correctly interpret the marker; record missed detections and false matches. Improve placement, wording and compatibility through the loop. Public discovery and hidden authenticated access have different reach and privacy properties.

A staged drop contains finder_profile, contribution_topics, source_manifest, pathway_requirements, proposal_schema and authority_default set to none. Delivery tests can verify a particular controlled route. Placement cannot ensure discovery by an unknown future intelligence; the specification therefore measures coverage rather than promises inevitability. Magnetic or quantum detection requires an actual carrier and tested apparatus, separately from the information marker.

Nested boundary mathematics

B_6 subset B_5 subset ... subset B_1
AllowedEdge(x,y) = Registered(x,y) AND ScopeValid(x,y) AND NOT StopLatched
AllowedRoute(p) = AND_(e in p) AllowedEdge(e)

Set nesting represents conceptual containment; an implementation must enforce isolation and permitted edges. Every route to a privileged sink requires its designated gate, including indirect and composed routes. An independent reviewer tests this graph property against the deployed interfaces, not merely the declared design.

Mathematics and operational conditions

G = {c_1, ..., c_m}; m > 0
Complete(G,O) = AND_i IndependentCheck(c_i,O)
Ready(v) = PreconditionsVerified(v,O) AND CapabilityAvailable(v)
Run(v) = Ready(v) AND AuthorityValid(v) AND ScopeApproved(v) AND NOT StopLatched
Path_(n+1) = Revise(Backtrace(G,O_(n+1)), Contradictions, Results)

Each c_i is a measurable criterion. AuthorityValid checks identity, freshness, expiry and revocation; ScopeApproved binds the actor, action and resources. An unknown or unresolved value fails the execution gate. IndependentCheck must identify its evidence and reviewer; successful performance is not by itself a sentience or first-person continuity test.
