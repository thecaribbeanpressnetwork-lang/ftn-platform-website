DEI AND INCLUSION

Use inclusion as product evidence, not application theatre.

Canadian and European programs often evaluate equity, diversity, gender or geographic inclusion. That can strengthen a proposal when ibis can show accessible design, Caribbean data representation, community participation, language inclusion and benefits for underserved groups. It does not erase applicant, country, company-control or consortium rules.

CANADA

Research partnerships

Canada's tri-agency framework includes EDI requirements in multiple programs. The practical route for FTN is normally a substantive partnership with a qualified Canadian university lead—not claiming that a Caribbean company can apply directly.

Official EDI statement · Global Affairs open calls

EUROPE

Consortium participation

Horizon Europe is open to international participation, but Trinidad and Tobago is not generally automatically funded. FTN should join only where the call explicitly provides funding or a consortium has a documented budget route.

Horizon Europe · EU–LAC participation FAQ

NOT A SHORTCUT

Women TechEU

The live program is for eligible women-led deep-tech startups in the EU or associated countries. FTN should never add a nominal leader or distort ownership to chase a weighting. It becomes relevant only if genuine leadership and establishment requirements are independently met.

CHINESE AI OPTIONS

Useful leverage—after Caribbean-specific evaluation.

FTN ibis should remain model-neutral. Open weights can reduce dependence on one US platform, but no model should receive Caribbean data merely because it is cheap or impressive on general benchmarks.

Model familyPotential FTN useGate before adoption
QwenApache-2.0 open weights, multilingual tool use and related open speech/multimodal families make it the strongest first Chinese evaluation candidate.Test Caribbean English, Creoles, Trinidad speech, citation fidelity, hallucination rate, compute cost and safe self-hosting.
DeepSeekReasoning and coding benchmark lane; some releases state commercial use subject to their model license.Review the exact model license, use restrictions, hosting jurisdiction, privacy terms and availability before production use.
GLMOpen agentic models with tool-calling support provide another portability and cost benchmark.Start with the smallest viable model and keep FTN provenance rules outside the model so a provider can be replaced.

The architecture remains: FTN-controlled data and permissions → FTN ibis MCP/API → evaluated models. Model vendors do not become owners of the Caribbean context graph.

90-DAY CAPITAL READINESS

Evidence before outreach volume

1. Product proof

Publish the ChatGPT plugin candidate and browser companion, then report verified tool calls, source opens, repeat use and errors without collecting raw conversations.

2. Regional validation

Secure one bounded pilot or research conversation with a Caribbean institution. Measure whether ibis finds and verifies opportunities more efficiently.

3. Investor data room

Prepare ownership, privacy, architecture, traction, market, unit economics, roadmap and use-of-funds evidence. Keep speculative consciousness research separate from product claims.

4. Targeted outreach

Approach five well-matched institutions or investors with a specific fit statement. Do not mass-email every organization on the map.
