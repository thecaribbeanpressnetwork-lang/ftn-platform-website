Accessibility Statement

Our accessibility target for the FTN Platform website, and how to tell us when we fall short of it.

Target
WCAG 2.2 Level AA
Applies to
Every page on this website
Found a gap?
Tell us what you found

The FTN Platform website is built to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA as its accessibility target. That means, among other things: every interactive control is reachable and operable by keyboard, color is never used as the only way to convey information, images carry meaningful alternative text, and form fields are properly labeled and associated with their error messages.

This is a target we build toward, not a guarantee that every page meets it perfectly today. If you encounter a page, component, or document that isn't accessible to you, we want to know about it.

Report an Issue

Tell us what you found.

Use our Contact page and select "Support" as the inquiry category, or describe the accessibility issue directly — include the page URL and, if possible, the assistive technology you were using. We'll follow up.
