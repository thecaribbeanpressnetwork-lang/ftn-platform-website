ibis

Continuity is a capability.

A conventional chatbot can answer a question well and still lose the larger story. ibis is being developed around the opposite requirement: maintain useful continuity across projects and time while still focusing tightly on the problem in front of it.

The goal is not to make memory larger. It is to make memory more useful to reasoning.

That means representing not only facts, but decisions, assumptions, alternatives, reasoning paths, outcomes and changes in belief. It also means knowing when a stored item is evidence, when it is inference, and when uncertainty should remain unresolved.

Applied capabilities under development include longitudinal context, causal and backward reasoning, opportunity identification, structured memory, personalized decision support and learning from prior reasoning paths rather than simply accumulating conversation text.

Founder Cognitive Layer

Preserve the path to a decision, not only the decision.

The Founder Cognitive Layer is a persistent architecture concept for recording how consequential decisions evolve: alternatives considered, assumptions in force, evidence available, causal pathways proposed, outcomes observed and lessons carried forward.

The practical research question is whether persistent agents can develop better continuity by retaining structured decision history and belief change instead of repeatedly rebuilding context from a shallow transcript. More speculative ideas about deeper cognitive preservation remain research hypotheses, not established science.

Research & Publications · Published preprint

From Memory to Causal History: Evidence-Bounded Retrodiction for Persistent Agents

Ricardo Gill · 2026 · Research Preprint · Zenodo · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22681856 ↗

In plain English: This research explores how an AI could do more than simply remember what happened. It proposes a method for an AI to work backward from what it sees now, examine the evidence available at different points in time, and reconstruct plausible chains of events that explain how and why the present situation came to be—while distinguishing facts from assumptions, preserving conflicting evidence, and admitting when there is not enough information to reach a reliable conclusion.

1

Historical record

What was documented or known at the time?

2

Access evidence

What evidence supports that an actor actually had access to information then?

3

Retrospective causal history

What can later evidence support about the mechanism that produced the outcome?

Technical contribution: Introduced Evidence-Bounded Retrodiction (EBR), a protocol for persistent AI agents that separates contemporaneous epistemic records, later evidence of historical actor access, and retrospective causal evidence. The framework combines temporal provenance, mechanism-gated causal reconstruction, contradiction preservation, open-set uncertainty, and explicit abstention, supported by an executable synthetic proof concept and falsifiable evaluation framework.

Why it matters: Most AI systems are designed primarily to answer questions from information they currently have. EBR investigates a different capability: giving persistent AI systems a disciplined way to reconstruct history, understand the reasoning and evidence surrounding past decisions, learn from those pathways, and avoid rewriting the past with information that only became available later.

Published September 9, 2026 as an independently authored, citable research preprint on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0. Publication as a preprint does not imply journal or peer-review acceptance.

Problems I want to solve

Future products begin with real friction.

These concepts are explicitly presented as concepts—not shipped products—because intellectual ambition is more credible when the boundary between vision and implementation stays clear.

Concept

Damaged-screen phone recovery interface

A legitimate owner connects a functioning phone with a damaged display to a computer, mirrors or interacts with the device, enters the phone’s normal password and retrieves personal data.

Security boundary: not a phone-unlocking or authentication-bypass product. The owner still supplies the device’s existing credentials.

Concept

Personal photo recovery & family archive

A tool to scan fragmented archives across old phones, drives and computers, identify a specified family member across different ages, and consolidate matching photographs into a durable archive.

The preferred direction emphasizes family-memory preservation, explicit owner control and local/private processing where feasible.
