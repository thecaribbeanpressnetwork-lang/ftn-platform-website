FTN Platform

Report. Connect. Improve.

One platform. Every community. Every voice. FTN Platform connects citizens and governments through Community Connect and Mission Control — building stronger, more transparent communities together.

No one sees the whole picture.

Citizens see their street. Councillors see their district. Ministries see their mandate. Every one of those views is real — and every one of them is partial. FTN Platform exists to connect the pieces: reality is the final authority, every observation is a clue, and every report a community sends in becomes part of a picture no single vantage point could see alone.

Why FTN Exists

Communities generate information every day. Most of it goes nowhere.

A pothole gets reported to no one in particular. A community meeting's decisions never reach the people they affect. A government agency wants to understand what's actually happening on the ground, but the data arrives too late, too filtered, or not at all.

FTN Platform was built to close that gap — giving citizens a direct, trackable way to report what they see, and giving governments a clear, structured view of what their communities are telling them. Two sides of the same problem, on one platform.

Platform Overview

One platform. Two audiences. One shared source of truth.

FTN Platform is the operating system connecting citizens and governments — Community Connect for the people reporting and following up, and Mission Control for the agencies acting on what's reported. Both draw from the same underlying reports, so nothing has to be re-entered or re-explained between the two.

Community Connect

Built for citizens. Built for change.

FTN Live

The nation's indicators, in one place, updated as it happens.

FTN Live is the public Observatory — a live-feeling wall of national indicators across the economy, energy, population, infrastructure, weather, and community, each one explained by a Trust Card so you can see exactly where a number comes from.

A clearer picture for the people responsible for the response.

Mission Control is the government-facing side of FTN Platform — an evidence-based decision-support environment built around the same reports citizens submit through Community Connect, so agencies see what's happening without re-entering anything.
