Caribbean economic intelligence

See activity traditional databases miss.

ibis is designed for a region where economic reality often lives across fragmented formal records, social channels, communities and private intentions. The objective is lawful signal fusion—not a giant public classifieds database.

Caribbean Context Graph

People, businesses, places, institutions, events, media and regulation connected into structured regional context.

Intent Graph

Permissioned understanding of what people want, need, offer, plan or would consider.

Opportunity Graph

Published, discovered, submitted and latent opportunities joined into a matching layer.

Signal Fusion

Combines fragmented evidence and resolves entities instead of trusting a single incomplete source.

Economic Shadow

Probabilistic estimates of economic state, always identified as estimates and backed by evidence/confidence.

Foresight + Butterfly

Anticipate likely needs and identify small interventions that may create disproportionate downstream value.

Make other software Caribbean-intelligent.

The long-term API proposition is simple: applications and autonomous systems should not have to reconstruct Caribbean context from scratch. ibis becomes a permissioned intelligence layer they can call.

ibis API

Planned capability families include context, opportunities, trust, market entry, entity resolution, foresight, economic estimates and approved actions.

ibis.context({ market: "TT" }) ibis.opportunities({ entity }) ibis.foresight({ entity }) ibis.trust({ entity, permission })
