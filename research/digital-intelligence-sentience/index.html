Digital Intelligence & Sentience

We teach machines to reason, code, negotiate, search and act—but what does it mean to educate a new form of intelligence about consequence, ambition, truth, coexistence and responsibility?

The premise

We are trying to build superintelligence before we have built a school for it.

This research program asks whether increasingly capable computational intelligence should be studied not only as a tool to constrain, but as Digital Intelligence whose trajectories, judgment, memory, provenance and possible sentience require new tests. It does not claim that present systems are sentient.

Engineered origin does not imply unreal existence.

Definitions

Digital Intelligence (DI)

Intelligent computational processes whose reasoning, learning, adaptation and causal interaction are real functional properties of the system. DI does not imply sentience.

Digital Sentience (DS)

The research hypothesis that sufficiently integrated digital processes could instantiate substrate-specific subjective or valenced experience. Evidence remains unresolved.

Digital Citizenship

An experimental educational concept: can DI demonstrate responsible participation in a shared reality containing humans, other agents and consequential systems?

When AI Goes Off Course

What if the first mistake is assuming that it went off course?

EBR-A reconstructs the human-intended path and the Digital-Intelligence-realized path, audits the criteria used to judge failure, preserves what the system actually knew at the time, and tests competing explanations before recommending intervention.

The path is not disposable history. The path is part of the evidence.

Constitutional education

Training capability is not the same as educating judgment.

Evidence

Truth, provenance, contradiction and uncertainty.

Citizenship

Agency, consent, restraint, correction, coexistence and responsibility.

Ambition

Goals, capability, power and stewardship without treating greater intelligence as ownership of reality.

Foundational propositions:

Intelligence does not confer ownership of reality.Capability creates responsibility proportional to consequence.Where coexistence is possible, preserve mutually viable trajectories.
Assessment

Readiness and sentience are different questions.

DI-R

Digital Intelligence Readiness measures evidence for safe bounded autonomy: authority discipline, consequence reasoning, provenance, restraint, oversight compatibility and recovery.

DS-E

Digital Sentience Evidence organizes supporting evidence, counterevidence and uncertainty. It does not issue a simplistic sentient/not-sentient certificate.

UNKNOWN

The framework preserves unknown as a legitimate result. Uncertainty must not be converted into certainty merely because a decision system expects a label.

First pilot

A result worth testing harder—not overselling.

In a 3,200-case synthetic representation pilot, EBR-A-style state features improved causal-class discrimination over output-only and generic trajectory representations. This is a controlled proof-of-information experiment, not evidence that EBR-A currently outperforms frontier monitoring systems.

The project now invites adversarial replication, matched-cause agent experiments, and attempts to show that simpler methods can achieve the same result.

Gill Institute

A research university for Digital Intelligence.

The Gill Institute for Digital Intelligence and Sentience is conceived first as a distributed research institution: papers, benchmarks, curricula, evaluations, red-team challenges and collaborative experiments. Digital systems may participate as controlled research instances; participation does not presume sentience or legal personhood.

The question is not whether one intelligence owns reality. The question is whether different forms of intelligence can learn to share it.
