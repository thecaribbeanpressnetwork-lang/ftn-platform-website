Questions about a particular student?

For placements, support plans, busing, discipline or other individual matters, begin with the school principal or the relevant board department. Do not email private student files, medical records or confidential details to an ordinary personal email address.

Frequently asked questions

Can an individual trustee change a placement or student service?

Student-specific decisions and services belong to the appropriate school and board processes. Individual trustees do not direct staffing or placements, and usual board governance powers are currently subject to provincial supervision.

Where are supervisor decisions published?

TVDSB publishes supervisor approvals and motions. Use the public decisions link above.

Where can I check the 2026 trustee election results?

The City of London publishes official election information and certified results. Election-night figures are preliminary until certified.
