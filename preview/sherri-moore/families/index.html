Before sending a student-specific concern

For placement, support plans, transportation, discipline or other individual matters, begin with the school principal or designated official board contact. Avoid sending medical records, identifying student information or confidential case files to a personal email address.

Common questions

Can a trustee direct my child's placement or provide a student service?

No individual trustee should be treated as a substitute for official school-board processes. Normal trustee governance authority is currently affected by provincial supervision. Use TVDSB's published contacts for individual cases.

Where can I see decisions made during supervision?

TVDSB publishes supervisor approvals and motions. See the linked decision log above.

Where can I check the latest election result?

Use the City of London's election resources. Media election-night reporting should not be confused with certified results.

Need an official contact?

Start with TVDSB contact information for schools, district offices and program services.
