Background

An education career and a record of community service.

Sherri describes a career at Western University beginning around 2000, with responsibilities spanning educational administration and policy work. She was appointed as a TVDSB trustee in 2021 and subsequently elected in 2022.

Her published biography also documents participation in advisory, community and school-board committees. Committee roles are presented as part of her service history rather than implying she currently holds decision-making authority under provincial supervision. [source] [source]

Current context

Understanding provincial supervision.

What changed at Thames Valley, who has decision-making authority and where families can find official information.

Important distinction: elected position and board authority

The Ontario government vested control of the Thames Valley District School Board in the Ministry of Education effective 23 April 2025. Its appointed supervisor oversees the board's financial and operational decisions. Election to a trustee position does not mean normal trustee powers have been restored. [source] [source]
