Meet Sherri

Education experience, a parent's perspective, public service.

Sherri has worked at Western University since 2000, with experience in educational administration, curriculum-related work and policy. She joined the Thames Valley District School Board as a trustee in 2021 and was elected in 2022.

A parent whose children attended Thames Valley schools, she brings firsthand experience with programs including French immersion, specialized learning supports, arts, co-op and dual credit. Her service also includes education advisory committees and London community organizations. ↗ ↗

What the current governance arrangements mean

Since 23 April 2025, the Ontario government has vested control of TVDSB administration in the Ministry of Education. The appointed supervisor exercises the board's financial and operational authority while supervision remains in effect. Elected trustees do not currently exercise their usual board powers. ↗ ↗
