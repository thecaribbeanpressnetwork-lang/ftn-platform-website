Supporting students with different needs

Thames Valley families depend on a range of programs and services, including special education, educational assistants and individualized supports. Staffing availability directly affects how those services are delivered. Recent reporting has documented concerns about shortages of educational assistants and the resulting pressure on students, families and school staff. ↗

Experience with special education governance

Sherri has served on the TVDSB Special Education Advisory Committee (SEAC), including as Vice-Chair. Her own family's experience across a wide range of school programs informs her engagement with these issues. ↗

“There are never enough EAs, and there have never been enough.”

— Sherri Moore, speaking about educational-assistant staffing in CBC coverage. ↗

Finding the right assistance

Parents seeking assistance with a child's placement, supports or learning plan should begin with the school and the relevant board services. Advisory processes operate within the current provincial supervision arrangements. The TVDSB contact directory is the starting point for official assistance.
