A safe learning environment

Safe, welcoming schools are places where students can learn without violence, discrimination or harassment. Discussions about safety include student wellbeing, bullying prevention and how schools work with community services.

Questions about school resource officers

In a 2022 CBC interview, Sherri questioned whether an armed school resource officer program was the right approach to creating safe, connected school communities. That interview forms part of the public record of her views at that time. ↗

“We want people to feel safe and connected with police, and that's great, but is this the only way we can do it?”

— Sherri Moore, CBC interview, 2022. ↗

How to raise a concern

Immediate safety concerns should be directed to emergency services or the school's emergency procedures. For non-emergency matters, begin with the school or the appropriate board department. The TVDSB contact directory contains official contacts.
