School safety has several dimensions

Concerns may involve physical safety, bullying, discrimination, student wellbeing and the relationship between schools and community services. A policy debate about one school-safety program should not be mistaken for a statement about every element of school safety.

Published comments on resource officers

In a CBC candidate-profile interview, Sherri raised questions about whether the former school resource officer program, involving armed police officers, was the appropriate model for schools. This was a documented position at the time of the interview, not an assumption about her future votes or decisions. [source]

Where to seek immediate assistance

For urgent safety concerns, use appropriate emergency services and official school-board reporting processes. This personal website is not a crisis response service. For routine concerns, contact the school using the district's published directory. [source]
