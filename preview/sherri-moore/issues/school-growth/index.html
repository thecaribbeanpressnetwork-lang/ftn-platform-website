Planning for a growing community

As London neighbourhoods grow, school capacity and access to child-care spaces become important planning questions. TVDSB publishes information about new facilities and capital projects, including the New Northwest Public School. ↗ ↗

New school investments

Plans announced for the Southwest and Northwest London elementary school projects included 1,606 student spaces and 176 child-care spaces combined. These are announced project figures; they are not a claim that every space is presently open. ↗

Sherri's involvement

Sherri publicly welcomed the Ministry's response to local school construction needs in the board's project announcement. Her work on planning and priorities has also formed part of her trustee committee service. ↗ ↗

Project information

For construction schedules, project milestones, school boundaries and opening information, consult the TVDSB capital projects pages and the Northwest Public School project information. Official updates remain the best source for changing timelines.
