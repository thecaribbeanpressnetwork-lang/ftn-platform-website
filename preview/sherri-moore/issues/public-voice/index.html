Elected representation and public decision-making

School board trustees are chosen through local elections, but board powers can change when the province intervenes under the Education Act. The TVDSB supervision order is a key official record of that change. [source]

What Sherri has said

Sherri's website expresses her concerns about community representation during provincial supervision and explains that she cannot access her former board email or perform official board functions. Those are her statements and should remain attributed to her. [source] [source]

What government records say

Ontario's oversight materials state that its decision followed an investigation into TVDSB financial administration. The government and individual trustees have expressed different interpretations of the causes, consequences and appropriate response. Readers should compare the provincial reports and trustees' own statements. [source] [source] [source]

See public decisions

TVDSB publishes a record of board motions and supervisor approvals, allowing members of the public to inspect available documents. [source]
