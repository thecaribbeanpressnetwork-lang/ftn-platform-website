Community representation matters

Trustees are elected by their communities to take part in the governance of public education. Sherri was appointed to the board in 2021 and elected in 2022; the October 2026 unofficial results also report her re-election. ↗ ↗

Representation during supervision

Under provincial supervision, trustees do not exercise their former board decision-making authority or use ordinary board communication channels. Sherri has described how this affects her ability to respond in an official capacity and why she continues to make herself available through a separate personal contact address. ↗ ↗

Understanding the provincial decision

The Ministry of Education says its intervention followed a financial investigation at TVDSB. Its published materials explain the government's findings and reasons for appointing a supervisor. Sherri has also expressed concerns about the effects of supervision on local accountability and community representation. Both sets of materials are available here for readers to examine. ↗ ↗ ↗

Access to public decisions

TVDSB publishes supervisor approvals and board motions. These records help families and community members follow decisions made during supervision. ↗
