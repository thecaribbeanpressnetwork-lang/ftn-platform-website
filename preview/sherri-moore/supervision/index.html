TVDSB under provincial supervision

A plain-language guide to the 2025 provincial order, changes to board authority and where to find current official decisions.

Home / TVDSB supervision
As of 27 October 2026: Ontario lists TVDSB under supervision. The statutory vesting of board authority began 23 April 2025. Check the ministry's current status before relying on this summary. [source] [source]

What happened?

  1. August 2024

    Public controversy over senior-official travel costs

    Ontario reports that an off-site retreat costing nearly $40,000 prompted public controversy and subsequent examination of TVDSB's finances. [source]

  2. April 2025

    Financial investigation and provincial order

    Ontario Order in Council 444/2025 states that an investigator's report, dated 11 April 2025, identified deficits and possible financial mismanagement. Control of TVDSB administration was vested in the Ministry effective 23 April. [source]

  3. 29 April 2025

    Supervisor announced

    The Ontario government announced Paul Boniferro to oversee the board's financial and operational decisions. [source]

  4. May–September 2026

    Legislative reforms

    The Putting Student Achievement First Act, 2026 received Royal Assent on 7 May; its provisions have differing commencement rules and orders. Refer to the statute for the legal text. [source]

What does it mean?

While provincial vesting remains in place, the elected board does not exercise its usual authority over TVDSB financial and operational matters. Families should continue to use official district channels for student-specific services and requests. [source] [source] [source]

Different public perspectives

Ontario's position: The Ministry's published overview describes financial-management concerns and states that its investigation led to a recommendation for control to be vested in the Ministry. [source]

Sherri's position: On her personal site, Sherri questions whether transferring power to provincial supervision addresses what she sees as systemic financial pressures and community accountability. This is her stated interpretation, not an independently established conclusion. [source]

Essential distinction

Provincial supervision means the usual authority of elected trustees has been displaced. Reporting an election victory does not mean a trustee is currently exercising those powers.
