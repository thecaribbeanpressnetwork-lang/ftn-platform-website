TVDSB under provincial supervision

An explanation of the provincial supervision order, trustee authority and how to find official decisions.

Home / TVDSB supervision
Current information · 27 October 2026

TVDSB remains under provincial supervision, which began on 23 April 2025. The Ministry of Education holds the board's administrative authority while the vesting order remains in effect. ↗ ↗

How supervision began

  1. August 2024

    Financial scrutiny

    An off-site retreat costing nearly $40,000 became the subject of public scrutiny, followed by examination of board finances. ↗

  2. April 2025

    Investigation and supervision

    After a financial investigation, Ontario vested control of TVDSB administration in the Ministry of Education effective 23 April 2025. ↗ ↗

  3. 29 April 2025

    Supervisor appointed

    The province announced Paul Boniferro as supervisor for the board's financial and operational decisions. ↗

  4. May 2026

    School board legislation changes

    The Putting Student Achievement First Act, 2026 received Royal Assent on 7 May 2026. Its provisions and commencement dates are set out in the statute. ↗

What this means for families

Schools and board departments remain the appropriate contacts for student-specific services. The supervisor exercises the board's governance authority; the elected trustees do not exercise their ordinary decision-making powers while the arrangement continues. Families can consult TVDSB's contact directory and the public supervisor decisions index. ↗ ↗ ↗

What the public record shows

The province identifies financial administration and governance concerns as reasons for intervention. Sherri has raised concerns about the loss of local representation and the effect of provincial supervision on community accountability. Her statements and the province's investigation are linked so readers can understand their respective positions. ↗ ↗ ↗
