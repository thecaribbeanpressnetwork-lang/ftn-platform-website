What a school board trustee does

What trustee representation means, what provincial supervision changes and how to reach the right school-board service.

Understanding the trustee role

School board trustees are elected to represent communities in public education governance. The legislation governing Ontario school boards defines what the board may do. An individual trustee cannot direct a principal to change a placement, assign staff or make decisions about a student's personal file.

What changed at Thames Valley

On 23 April 2025, Ontario vested control of TVDSB administration in the Ministry of Education. A provincially appointed supervisor now oversees financial and operational decisions while the order remains in effect. The usual decision-making authority of trustees is therefore restricted. ↗ ↗

How I can be contacted

I remain available through my personal contact address for general questions and community correspondence. I can help identify publicly available information and the appropriate official channels, but this contact is separate from the school board. My personal email is not a substitute for a school office, a formal student-service process or an emergency service. ↗

Where to start with a school concern

For student placement, special education supports, transportation, discipline or other individual matters, start with the school principal or the relevant TVDSB department. The TVDSB contact directory provides the district's official communication routes. The supervisor decisions index provides public governance records.

Privacy and student information

Do not email private student records, medical documents or identifying information about children to an ordinary personal email address. Ask the board how to submit sensitive information securely.
