What a trustee can — and cannot — do

Trustee elections, board governance, and everyday school services are related but distinct.

Home / Trustee role

What is a school board trustee?

School board trustees are elected representatives in Ontario's public education governance system. The duties and powers of the board are defined by legislation; an individual trustee generally cannot independently direct schools, assign staff or decide a student's placement.

What changed at Thames Valley?

Under Ontario Order in Council 444/2025, control and charge over TVDSB administration were vested in the Ministry of Education effective 23 April 2025. The Ministry appointed Paul Boniferro as supervisor to oversee financial and operational decisions. [source] [source]

What can families do?

For questions about specific students, families should begin with the school's official administrative contacts, then use the board's published escalation or services channels. For public governance information, Ontario's oversight page and TVDSB's board motions remain useful reference points. [source] [source]

Where Sherri fits in

Sherri's published website describes her continuing availability through a separate personal contact email. It is a personal point of contact, not a TVDSB office or an official school-board service. Any assistance she can offer is subject to her actual current role and applicable restrictions. [source]

A note on individual student information

Do not email confidential education, health or child-protection documents to an unofficial address. Ask the appropriate school-board office how to transmit sensitive records securely.
