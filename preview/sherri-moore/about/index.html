Professional background

Sherri Moore is a Thames Valley District School Board trustee representing London Wards 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13 in Ontario. According to her published account, she moved to London around 2000 and built a career in educational administration at Western University, including work on curriculum support, registration, and policy development and review. [source]

The board appointed her to fill a trustee vacancy in December 2021. She was subsequently elected in October 2022. Reporting on the 26 October 2026 municipal election lists her among the two trustee candidates with the highest vote totals in her area; final certification should be checked with the City of London. [source] [source] [source]

Education-sector experience

Her career has involved administrative and policy responsibilities within Western University. This background is distinct from classroom teaching; no claim of classroom teaching credentials is made here. In her current online biography, Sherri says she completed graduate study in Equity, Diversity and Social Justice and a communications certificate. The exact degree and award status must be confirmed with her before final publication because the supplied research package contains older, conflicting wording. [source]

Family perspective

Sherri's public biography explains that her children attended TVDSB schools and that the family encountered a wide range of education pathways, including French immersion, special education, dual credit, co-op, arts programming, and specialized transportation. This experience forms part of her own account of why she became involved in school governance. [source]

Public and community involvement

Her published service history includes the Special Education Advisory Committee, First Nations Advisory Committee, Planning and Priorities Advisory Committee and other school-board bodies, alongside community organizations such as Autism Ontario's London Chapter and Pride London Festival. These are historical service records; current committee membership and authority require independent confirmation while the board remains under supervision. [source]
