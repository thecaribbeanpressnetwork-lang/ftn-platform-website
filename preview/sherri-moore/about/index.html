About Sherri Moore

More than two decades in education administration, firsthand family experience and years of community and school board involvement.

Home / About Sherri

Experience in education

Sherri Moore is an education professional and Thames Valley District School Board trustee representing London Wards 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13. Since moving to London in 2000, she has built her professional career at Western University. Her work has included curriculum support, program registration, administrative coordination, and the drafting, review and administration of policy. ↗

Her experience spans the systems, procedures and everyday work that support post-secondary education. It is a substantial professional background alongside her school board and community service.

From community involvement to school board service

In December 2021, Sherri was appointed to the Thames Valley board to fill a trustee vacancy. Voters elected her in October 2022. The October 26, 2026 unofficial election results also list her among the two elected trustees for Wards 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13, alongside Lincoln McCardle. ↗ ↗ ↗

Her school board service has included the Planning and Priorities Advisory Committee, Special Education Advisory Committee (SEAC), First Nations Advisory Committee, Discipline Committee, Negotiations Advisory Committee and Thames Valley Education Foundation. She has also served with the Ontario Public School Boards' Association, including as a director and, from June 2026, Western Region Vice-Chair. ↗

A parent's perspective

Sherri's three children attended Thames Valley schools. Along the way, her family navigated French immersion, autism spectrum disorder programming, gifted programming, Beal Art, dual credit, co-op and specialized transportation. These are not abstract topics to her: they are among the educational pathways her own family experienced. ↗

Commitment to community

Her community involvement has included Autism Ontario's London Chapter, Rainbow Network London (formerly the HBT Working Group), Pride London Festival and London Middlesex Roller Derby. In 2018, she received the Ken Sadler Award for Individual Contribution. ↗

Why this work matters

“I see my role at the school board as a call to service. It feels right to me to be putting my energy into this work. I love to solve problems. Ask questions. Dig for answers.”

— Sherri Moore
