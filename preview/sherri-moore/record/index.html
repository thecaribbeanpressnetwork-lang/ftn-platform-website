A dated public-service record

The timeline distinguishes historical appointments and service from current governing authority. Each event is supported by a published biography, school-board record or external report.

  1. 2018

    Community recognition

    Sherri's published biography lists the Ken Sadler Award for Individual Contribution. Award wording and issuer should be confirmed before promotional use. [source]

  2. December 2021

    Appointed as trustee

    TVDSB selected Sherri to fill a vacancy on its elected board. Contemporary reporting described an appointment process involving multiple applicants. [source]

  3. October 2022

    Elected to the Thames Valley board

    Sherri won election to represent London Wards 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13. [source]

  4. 2022–2025

    Committees and education governance

    Her public biography lists committee service, including planning, special education and other advisory responsibilities. For accurate historical records, the precise appointment and end dates should be verified with the board. [source]

  5. 23 April 2025

    TVDSB placed under provincial supervision

    The province vested the board's authority in the Ministry of Education. Board decision-making powers were no longer exercised by the elected trustees. [source]

  6. 7 May 2026

    Ontario enacted Bill 101

    The Putting Student Achievement First Act, 2026 received Royal Assent. The amendments address several aspects of school-board governance and provincial authority; some provisions have distinct commencement dates. [source]

  7. 26 October 2026

    Reported re-election

    Election-night reporting lists Sherri Moore and Lincoln McCardle as the two candidates with the highest vote totals for London Wards 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13. The report was unofficial at publication; check the City of London for certification. [source] [source]

An election result is not the same as restored trustee decision-making authority. For the practical implications, read TVDSB supervision.
