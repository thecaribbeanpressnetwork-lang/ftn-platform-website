Milestones in public service

Sherri's path to the school board grew from years of work in education and involvement with London community organizations. This timeline brings together significant dated milestones and links to the underlying public records.

  1. 2018

    Ken Sadler Award

    Received the Ken Sadler Award for Individual Contribution, recognizing community involvement. ↗

  2. December 2021

    Appointed TVDSB trustee

    Selected to fill a trustee vacancy on the Thames Valley District School Board. ↗

  3. 2022–2026

    Provincial association service

    Served as a director of the Ontario Public School Boards' Association. Sherri's board and advisory committee experience includes planning and special education. ↗

  4. October 2022

    Elected school board trustee

    Elected to represent London Wards 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13, receiving 6,900 votes in that contest. ↗

  5. 23 April 2025

    Provincial supervision begins

    Ontario vested control of TVDSB administration in the Ministry of Education, changing the authority exercised by the elected board. ↗

  6. June 2026

    OPSBA Western Region leadership

    Elected Vice-Chair for the Western Region of the Ontario Public School Boards' Association.

  7. 26 October 2026

    Re-elected in election-night results

    Unofficial municipal election results list Sherri Moore and Lincoln McCardle as the two elected trustees for Wards 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13. The City of London is the authority for certified results. ↗ ↗

Current governance: The election result does not restore ordinary school board powers while provincial supervision remains in effect. Read the supervision explanation.
