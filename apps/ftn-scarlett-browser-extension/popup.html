Turning this on lets Scarlett see which sites this page talks to (analytics, advertising, social and unknown third parties) on every site you visit, and optionally block known trackers. This stays entirely on your device: Scarlett never uploads what it observes, and FTN receives nothing from it. You can turn it off at any time, which removes the permission entirely.
