Your browser can be a research tool

Search normally in Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo, then let ibis reason over the visible results. Cookies, login credentials and search history stay in your browser.

Browser context is captured only when you press “Include browser search”. FTN ibis receives the visible result titles, links and snippets — never your Google cookies, passwords, account tokens or browsing history.
