FTN Trust Centre

What happens, what doesn’t, and who controls it.

A plain-language map of FTN’s current public sources, product states, local storage, accounts, protected boundaries, corrections and external providers. Last operational review: 2026-08-25.

Product truth

LIVE, AVAILABLE, PRIVATE, VAULTED

The Product Registry drives public state. AVAILABLE means a functioning public journey with disclosed limits. PRIVATE requires authorized access. VAULTED work is retained internally but excluded from public discovery until a separate release gate passes.

Sources

Follow-through, not implied endorsement

Current public claims identify a source and checked date where appropriate. Official records remain owned by their source authority; FTN context is labelled separately.

Private boundaries

Separate by purpose

Community Connect, vaulted products, ibis private content, authentication and owner controls are not placed in the public offline cache or reused silently across products.

AI

Method and provider visibility

Deterministic routing is not called generative AI. ibis shows local/on-device/server method boundaries. Generated output is a draft until a user reviews and publishes it. When a response draws on a real live source, its own compact evidence card names that source, shows when the source itself was published (separately from when FTN retrieved it), how it was retrieved, and a real confidence basis — or the explicit label “Not assessed,” never a percentage that isn’t real. A fallback or unavailable route is always disclosed, not hidden. Hidden prompts, credentials, internal provider retry attempts and private conversation content are never shown in that card or retained beyond an ordinary reply.

Browser storage

Preferences and local work

Country choice, last public task, drafts, saved sources and some creative projects may live in your browser. They are not cloud backups. Private PWA caching is disabled.

Analytics

Cloudflare delivery telemetry

The production host currently exposes Cloudflare Web Analytics/delivery telemetry. FTN does not use that as permission for sensitive replay, cross-site advertising or unrelated product profiling.

Corrections and rights

Challenge a claim or source.

Report a broken source, copyright issue, privacy concern, accessibility barrier or inaccurate FTN statement with the affected URL and enough detail to investigate. Do not email passwords, medical records, private civic evidence or identity documents.
