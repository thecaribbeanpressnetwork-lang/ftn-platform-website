Mission Control

A clearer picture for the people responsible for the response.

Mission Control is the government-facing side of FTN Platform — a dashboard built to help agencies see what communities are reporting through Community Connect, understand where issues are concentrated, and track how they're being resolved.

The capabilities described on this page reflect Mission Control's current product design. Availability and feature scope may vary by agency and deployment — contact our government team for specifics relevant to your agency.
Government Dashboard

One dashboard, six views into the same data.

Executive Overview

A high-level summary of report volume, status, and resolution rate — see the Executive Brief section below.

Reports Management

Browse, filter, and manage incoming citizen reports as they arrive.

Analytics & Trends

Trends in report volume and category over time — see the Analytics section below.

Map Intelligence

A geographic view of where reports are concentrated — see Situational Awareness below.

Agency Performance

Performance data scoped to a specific agency's area of responsibility — see Agency Views below.

System Monitor

Operational status of the platform itself, for administrators.

Executive Brief

A summary built for leadership, not analysts.

The Executive Overview is designed to answer the questions leadership actually asks: how many reports came in, how many are still open, and how quickly they're being resolved — without requiring anyone to build their own report first.

Agency Views

Scoped to what each agency is actually responsible for.

Agency Performance views are designed to be scoped — an agency sees the reports and performance data relevant to its own area of responsibility, rather than a city-wide or national feed it has no mandate to act on.

Analytics

Trends, not just totals.

Analytics & Trends is designed to show how report volume and category shift over time — using line, bar, and category-breakdown views — so agencies can see whether a given issue is growing, shrinking, or seasonal, rather than reading a single report in isolation.

Situational Awareness

Where issues are, not just what they are.

Map Intelligence is designed to plot reports geographically, so agencies can see where issues are concentrated across a community — useful for spotting patterns that a list of individual reports wouldn't show on its own.

Platform Security

Built around the same trust principles as the rest of the platform.

Mission Control is designed with government use in mind: access is intended to be scoped by role and agency, so users see only what's relevant to their own responsibilities. Security and compliance requirements vary by government and jurisdiction — if your agency needs detailed security documentation as part of an evaluation, our government team can provide it directly.
