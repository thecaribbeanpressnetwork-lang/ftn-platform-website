Mission Control

A clearer picture for the people responsible for the response.

Mission Control is the government-facing side of FTN Platform — a dashboard built to help agencies see what communities are reporting through Community Connect, understand where issues are concentrated, and track how they're being resolved.

The capabilities described on this page reflect Mission Control's current product design. Availability and feature scope may vary by agency and deployment — contact our government team for specifics relevant to your agency.
Executive Operations Centre

Reality is the final authority. Every observation is a clue.

Mission Control is an evidence-based decision-support environment for authorized leaders — a single operations view that turns community reports into an executive picture, live relationships, and evidence-backed recommendations.

From the public Interactive Demonstration — illustrative data, the real interface.

Capabilities

Eight ways to work with the same evidence.

Every capability below is live in the public Interactive Demonstration today — built with clearly labelled demonstration data, not the secure production application. Select any card to open it directly.

Executive Dashboard

A leadership-level read on infrastructure pressure, household pressure, service reliability, and agency workload — the questions leadership actually asks, answered without building a report first.

Correlation Engine

The same shared Relationship Engine used on FTN Live — indicators linked by correlation, influence, and dependency, scoped to what a given response is actually connected to.

Reality Graph

An interactive node graph tracing how one factor (rainfall, say) chains into flooding, road access, and economic output — click any node to follow the chain.

Scenario Studio

Adjust a lever and see the projected, illustrative effect on related indicators — every relationship is a hypothesis, every intervention framed as an experiment.

Evidence Explorer

The chain of evidence behind a recommendation — source records, community observations, related news, and historical precedent, laid out in order.

Strategic Advisor

Set an outcome, an area, a budget band, and a time horizon — get a situation summary and contributing factors grounded in the same evidence base.

Timeline & Memory

How a situation has moved over time, so a decision is informed by trend, not a single snapshot.

External Influence

Factors outside the platform's own reports — regional and international context that shapes what's happening locally.

Platform Security

Built around the same trust principles as the rest of the platform.

Mission Control is designed with government use in mind: access is intended to be scoped by role and agency, so users see only what's relevant to their own responsibilities. Security and compliance requirements vary by government and jurisdiction — if your agency needs detailed security documentation as part of an evaluation, our government team can provide it directly.
