Meet Amari

Same gyal. Different vibes.

Amari Inniss is a human-directed virtual artist from Trinidad & Tobago. Her sound moves across Groovy Soca, Power Soca, Soca Jab, Reggae, Chutney-influenced Soca, Trini Hip Hop, Dancehall and Trinibad.

She is designed to feel recognisably Caribbean rather than generically “AI”: warm, confident, playful, grown, rooted in Trinidad & Tobago culture and built around good music, good people and good energy.

Amari is fictional. The creative decisions are not. Her music and public identity are developed under human direction, with Rick Boss leading the writing, arrangement, production choices, refinement, mixing, mastering and release strategy.

More than music… it's us.

How Amari was created

AI helped make Amari possible. Rick Boss made the decisions that make her Amari.

Writing & arrangement

Rick Boss writes and arranges the songs, defines the concepts and decides how each record should move, build and land.

Voice & identity direction

Amari's voice, look and performance identity were developed through repeated human direction, selection, rejection and refinement — not a one-click generation.

Production decisions

Generated material is treated as source material. Rick chooses versions, works with stems, adjusts musical elements and protects the Trinidad & Tobago character of the records.

Mixing, mastering & quality control

The release masters were completed manually in Ableton Live under human creative and technical control, with repeated listening, correction and album-wide QC.

Ownership & release

Rick controls the final creative decisions, metadata, release strategy and project direction. AI is part of the toolkit; it is not the autonomous owner, manager or decision-maker.
