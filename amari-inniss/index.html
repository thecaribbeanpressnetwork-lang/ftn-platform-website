Meet Amari

Same gyal. Different vibes.

Amari Inniss is a human-directed virtual artist from Trinidad & Tobago. Her music moves through Groovy Soca, Power Soca, Soca Jab, Reggae, Chutney-influenced Soca, Trini Hip Hop, Dancehall and Trinibad.

She is built to feel recognisably Caribbean rather than generically “AI”: warm, confident, playful, grown and rooted in Trinidad & Tobago culture.

Amari is a virtual artist, but the creative decisions behind her are human-led. Rick Boss leads the writing, arrangement, production choices, voice and visual refinement, manual mixing and mastering, quality control and release direction.

More than music… it's us.

The human behind Amari

AI expanded the toolbox. Rick Boss remained the creative decision-maker.

Writing & arrangement

Rick Boss writes and arranges the songs, defines the concepts and decides how each record should move, build and land.

Selection & refinement

Generated options are auditioned, rejected, reshaped and refined. Voice, phrasing, structure and cultural feel are not left to chance.

Production & engineering

Rick works with stems, musical elements and arrangement choices, then manually mixes and masters in Ableton Live under human technical control.

Final authority

Quality control, metadata, artwork direction, release strategy and the final “yes” or “no” remain human decisions.
