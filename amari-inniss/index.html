Meet Amari

Same gyal. Different vibes.

Amari Inniss is a Trinidad & Tobago artist moving through Groovy Soca, Power Soca, Soca Jab, Reggae, Chutney-influenced Soca, Trini Hip Hop, Dancehall and Clean Trinibad.

Warm, confident, playful and grown, her world is built around music, Carnival, friendship, everyday Trinidad life and the kind of energy that feels familiar before it feels famous.

The artist comes first. For people interested in how the project is made, the BeTT section below documents the team, production standards and technology behind the music.

How BeTT makes the music
Boss Entertainment TT (BeTT)

Technology expanded the toolbox. The BeTT team remains responsible for the standard.

Concept & arrangement

Every record starts with a clear musical idea. The BeTT team develops the concept, arrangement and performance direction until the song has its own identity.

Selection & refinement

Options are auditioned, rejected, reshaped and refined. Voice, phrasing, structure, groove and cultural feel are reviewed as production decisions, not left to chance.

Production & engineering

BeTT works through stems, musical elements, edits and arrangement choices, with mixing, mastering and final QC carried out under human technical control.

Final authority

Quality control, metadata, artwork direction, release strategy and the final “yes” or “no” remain human decisions.
