Why discretion matters

A premature disclosure can move a negotiation, expose a business plan, or turn a policy discussion into a public commitment. People also give more candid advice when they know who will have access to it.

We publish our research and point to our public work. We keep client identities, engagements and discussions private. That discretion goes with clear accountability and any applicable disclosure obligations.
