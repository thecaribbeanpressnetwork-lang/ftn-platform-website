CONTENT & PLAYBACK RULES

DJ-owned drops: allowed and stored/played by the DJ profile.

Other DJ mixes: excluded from indexing/recommendations when the title contains standalone “mix”; we do not download or rehost them.

YouTube ads: DJ Tube must not bypass, block, or automate ad skipping. We can detect the actual music start after permitted playback and cue there.

Song start detection: production can identify silence, spoken intro, title-card material and the first strong musical phrase and use that as the cue point, subject to what the permitted playback interface exposes.
