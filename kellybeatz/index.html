03 / The producer

Roots in gospel.
Room to move.

From Point Fortin to Charlotte, North Carolina: a musician’s ear, a producer’s instinct.

Kelly grew up playing drums in church. Time between Trinidad and the United States introduced the hip-hop and R&B influences that sit alongside his gospel foundation. He moved into dancehall in 2018, building records with Rebel Sixx and Boy Boy before credits including Prince Swanny’s Catch 22 and K Lion’s Say.

“I just mix all my influences together.”Kelly Beatz · 2020 interview

Musicality before categories.

Gospel, hip-hop, R&B and dancehall inform the same production vocabulary.

One rhythm. Individual voices.

A riddim creates a shared foundation; the artist’s performance gives each record its character.

Build it. Mix it. Finish it.

Production, mixing and mastering meet on records including Uncrowned King.
