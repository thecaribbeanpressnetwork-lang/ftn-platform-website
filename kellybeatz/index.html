03 / The producer

Roots in gospel.
Room to move.

Joshua Kelly brings a musician’s foundation to Trinidad’s dancehall landscape.

In a 2020 interview, Kelly described a production approach shaped by gospel, hip-hop and R&B, and a move into dancehall in 2018. His work connects melodic sensibility with the energy of Trinibad.
