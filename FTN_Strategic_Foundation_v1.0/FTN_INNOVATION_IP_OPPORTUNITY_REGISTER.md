## Current Opportunities

### IP-001 — Distance-Aware Rendering
Date recorded: July 2026  
Problem: Conventional responsive design considers viewport size but not how far the audience stands from the display.  
Concept: User supplies or hardware infers average viewing distance; presentation automatically adjusts type size, density, chart complexity, timing, contrast, icon scale and information count.  
Applications: Banks, airports, outdoor screens, hospitals, corporate lobbies, education, broadcast.  
Potential value: Premium display feature, licensing, hardware partnerships.  
Status: Concept; architecture requested.

### IP-002 — FTN Presentation Engine
Date recorded: July 2026  
Problem: Separate codebases for mobile, web, television, portrait displays and broadcast create duplication.  
Concept: A shared engine uses presentation profiles to generate context-appropriate layouts from common data, source and indicator registries.  
Status: Partial foundations exist.

### IP-003 — Adaptive Information Density Engine
Concept: Automatically calculate how much information can be shown based on distance, screen size, orientation, audience, reading level, dwell time and cognitive load.

### IP-004 — Reading Speed Engine
Concept: Slide duration is calculated from word count, visual complexity, audience profile and comprehension buffer instead of fixed timers.

### IP-005 — Cognitive Load Engine
Concept: Score and constrain the mental effort of each screen, then adjust density, wording, motion and simultaneous elements.

### IP-006 — Audience Intelligence Profiles
Concept: Present the same truth differently for children, teenagers, citizens, executives, tourists, researchers, patients and corporate audiences without altering the underlying data.

### IP-007 — Sunlight Presentation Mode
Concept: High-ambient-light profile with stronger contrast, simplified graphics, larger type, thicker lines and reduced subtle effects for outdoor displays.

### IP-008 — Two-Statistic Story Mode
Concept: Display two related statistics at a time, hold them beyond average reading time, then advance through a structured national narrative.

### IP-009 — Weighted Random Display Rotation
Concept: Randomized screen rotation that preserves campaign obligations, novelty, repetition limits, audience relevance and information priority.

### IP-010 — Memory-Based Rotation Engine
Concept: Track what each screen has displayed and reduce fatigue by balancing novelty with necessary repetition.

### IP-011 — National Information Broadcasting Platform
Concept: A shared platform produces public web, commercial displays and continuous broadcast streams from one indicator and presentation engine.

### IP-012 — Trust Card System
Concept: Every data object exposes source, benchmark, freshness, classification, methodology, confidence, assumptions, limitations and relationships through a consistent interface.

### IP-013 — Reality Object Model
Concept: Indicators are not isolated numbers; each object carries source, time, geography, relationships, method, history, confidence and display behaviour.

### IP-014 — Relationship-Aware National Observatory
Concept: Public indicators reveal what influences them and what they influence, allowing non-experts to explore national systems.

### IP-015 — Contextual Public Display Advertising
Concept: Advertising is selected by venue, audience, time, content profile and customer package without personal surveillance.

### IP-016 — QR Companion Display System
Concept: Public screen remains clean while a viewer scans a QR code to inspect sources, charts, methodology, save or share the item.

### IP-017 — Local Benchmark Counter Engine
Concept: Scheduled benchmark acquisition feeds local per-second animation, avoiding continuous requests to many source sites.

### IP-018 — Calibration and Pace-Correction Engine
Concept: Compare current model totals with newly published benchmarks and adjust future rates while preserving historical versions and error records.

### IP-019 — Seasonal National Weighting Engine
Concept: Apply documented Trinidad and Tobago calendar, holiday, Carnival, school-term, weather-season and event profiles to annual-rate counters.

### IP-020 — Question-First Strategic Advisor
Concept: AI must ask qualifying questions about objective, geography, budget, time, constraints and affected groups before presenting evidence-backed options.

### IP-021 — Contradiction Engine
Concept: Detect and present conflicts between official claims, citizen observations, news reports and later outcomes.

### IP-022 — Missing Data Engine
Concept: Explicitly measure and present what is unknown, stale or absent rather than treating missing data as zero.

### IP-023 — National Memory
Concept: Preserve dated national snapshots, interventions, decisions, predictions and outcomes for future comparison and institutional learning.

### IP-024 — Recommendation Memory
Concept: Track whether recommendations were acted on and compare expected with actual outcomes.

### IP-025 — Butterfly Effect Visualization
Concept: Interactive chains showing how small local observations or interventions may connect to broader social, economic and institutional outcomes.
