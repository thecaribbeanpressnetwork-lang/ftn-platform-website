# FTN Revenue Model

Version: 1.0  
Status: Founder Working Draft

## Purpose

Define how the FTN ecosystem can generate early, recurring and scalable income without depending on retail inventory or compromising trust.

## Core Revenue Principles

1. Recurring revenue before one-off revenue.
2. Access, intelligence, distribution and curation before retail.
3. Free public value can support paid institutional value.
4. Advertising should subsidize access, not corrupt information.
5. Premium customers pay for control, customization, service, exclusivity, reliability and reduced advertising.
6. No pricing should be finalized until real customer discovery and cost modelling are completed.

## Advertising Tiers

### Free Public
- Highest network advertising
- FTN promotions
- Public-service messages
- No guaranteed customization

### Sponsored Display
- Lower subscription
- Significant network advertising
- Customer-selected indicators

### Standard Display
- Moderate advertising
- More customer control
- Customer promotion allowance

### Premium Display
- Limited external ads
- Customer campaigns
- Priority layouts

### Ad-Free Display
- No third-party ads
- FTN identity remains
- Highest standard subscription tier

### Enterprise / Government
- Custom ad policy
- Customer-only content
- No network ads unless approved
- Premium service
