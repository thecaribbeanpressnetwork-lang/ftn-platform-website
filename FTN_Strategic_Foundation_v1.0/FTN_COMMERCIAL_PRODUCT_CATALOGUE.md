## Product Rules

- Public trust products must remain useful without payment.
- Paid products should add capability, control, service, depth, or reduced advertising.
- Advertising should never obscure or distort evidence.
- Mission Control remains private and permission-controlled.
- Products should share engines and data models rather than duplicate systems.
- Revenue should favour recurring, scalable models.
- No product should be launched merely because it can be built.
