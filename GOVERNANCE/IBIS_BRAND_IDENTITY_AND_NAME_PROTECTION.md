## Brand decision

The primary public and ownership-facing brand is **FTN ibis**. The available-name strategy adds a family of compound identifiers so the project does not depend on exclusive ownership of the crowded bare word “ibis.”

The name “ibis” is memorable, regional, intelligent and compatible with a Caribbean bird symbol. It is also a common word and may already be used by unrelated companies, products, hotels, software, domains or marks. Therefore the defensible ownership strategy is not to assume that the bare word is globally exclusive. It is to build and protect a distinctive family of FTN-owned compound marks, visual assets, product architecture and dated evidence.

## Recommended use of the available variants

Use **ibis by FTN Platform** in formal public, funding and ownership contexts. Use **Tell ibis** in interface copy and voice commands: “Tell ibis what you want to make happen.” Use **ibisAI** in technical documentation, APIs and repositories where a compact namespace is useful. Use **Tellibis** for a consumer-facing domain or handle if it clears. Keep **TellibisAI** as a fallback and do not lead with all variants at once; too many public names would weaken recognition.

## Primary visual identity

### Mark concept

The primary mark is a minimal ibis eye/wing signal: a dark circular field, a warm gold eye/feather signal and a small blue Caribbean horizon accent. It should suggest:

- an ibis without becoming a literal wildlife illustration;
- attention and perception;
- a signal entering a reasoning field;
- Caribbean warmth without using a national flag as decoration;
- a calm, premium intelligence system rather than a cartoon assistant.

### Wordmark

Use lower-case **ibis** in a clean geometric sans. Lower-case communicates approachability and an always-present intelligence layer. Use **ibis by FTN Platform** in formal contexts, investor material and ownership records.

### Palette

- Obsidian: `#07090D` — foundation, trust, depth.
- Graphite: `#111722` — working surfaces.
- Signal gold: `#D4A85C` — insight, value, attention.
- Caribbean blue: `#94B9FF` — context, evidence, horizon.
- Integrity green: `#63C58A` — verified/healthy status only.
- Alert coral: `#E58A76` — warning or blocked state only.

### Behavior

The mark should have an ambient presence, a meaningful reveal when Headspace assembles, and a restrained completion signal when a decision is saved or verified. No flashing, addictive loop or covert persuasion.
