# FTN Platform — Final Independent Audit and Repair Decision

**Date:** 24 August 2026
**System reviewed:** https://ftnplatform.org/
**Review type:** Consolidated live-product, architecture, evidence, market and monetization audit
**Audits consolidated:** Claude, Gemini, Grok, ChatGPT Plus, ChatGPT Free, Perplexity, Mistral and Kimi
**Changes made during audit:** None

This message contains the actual audit findings. It is not a reference to a missing attachment.

## 1. Executive verdict

FTN Platform is a real, coherent Caribbean digital ecosystem with functioning products and shared architectural foundations. It is not merely a prototype.

Its strongest assets are:

* its connected-product concept;
* Caribbean positioning;
* premium black visual foundation;
* source-conscious civic posture;
* Community Connect's consent boundary;
* separation between FTN Govern, Parliament and official institutions;
* functioning FTN DAW, Radio and scenario controls;
* emerging provenance and Trust Card architecture.

The platform is not yet ready to be marketed as fully dependable institutional infrastructure.

Its most serious weakness is the gap between what some products appear to promise and what their data freshness, intelligence routing, mathematical relationships and completion paths can prove.

The site should not be rebuilt. It should be repaired, consolidated and completed.

**Final strategic classification: BUILD NOW.**

The repair must achieve four outcomes:

1. every public number becomes defensible;
2. every core journey reaches a useful completion state;
3. overlapping products become one intentional architecture;
4. FTN Statistics and ibis become shared ecosystem infrastructure rather than additional silos.

## 4. Accepted critical and blocker findings

### FTN-FINAL-001 — Stale murder statistic presented with false freshness confidence

FTN correctly copied a TTPS figure of 120 recorded murders.

However, the underlying TTPS page appears materially stale and has no reliable visible "as at" date.

Independent reporting cited approximately:

* 213 murders by 20 July 2026;
* 228 murders by 12 August 2026.

FTN displayed:

* the 120 figure;
* a Trust Score of 92/100;
* recent "last updated" language.

The recent date appears to describe when FTN retrieved or processed the page—not when the underlying statistic was current.

This proves that an official source can still be stale.

Required repair:

* suppress the figure from current-condition presentation until its statistical reference period is established;
* separate source authority from source freshness;
* never use FTN retrieval time as the data's update time;
* add cross-source discrepancy detection;
* reduce the Trust Score automatically when the source lacks an "as at" date;
* distinguish reported/recorded crime from total underlying crime;
* prevent stale public-safety data from appearing live merely because the publisher is official.

### FTN-FINAL-002 — Unsupported FTN Display debt animation

FTN Display showed a debt value increasing by approximately TTD 620 per second.

Its Trust Card reportedly disclosed:

* an assumed borrowing rate;
* an official figure still pending;
* a warning that the result should not be cited.

It still received approximately 83/100 trust.

Required repair:

* remove the moving debt calculation;
* do not restore it until there is:

  * an official baseline;
  * a statistical reference date;
  * a defensible formula;
  * correct units;
  * FTN Math Mode;
  * a direct source;
  * a justified confidence score.
* Ensure Trust Scores represent evidence quality—not interface confidence.

### FTN-FINAL-003 — ibis cannot perform promised national research

Claude found that guest ibis is gated and falls back to deterministic product routing.

ChatGPT Plus reached a research pathway, but a Trinidad and Tobago state-of-emergency investigation searched Hacker News and GitHub instead of CSO, TTPS or authoritative national sources.

These findings can coexist through different authentication states or routes. Neither demonstrated dependable authoritative national intelligence.

Required repair:

* create an authoritative-source registry;
* prioritize official Trinidad and Tobago and Caribbean sources;
* expose source class and freshness;
* add lawful fallback retrieval;
* separate evidence from inference;
* never use Hacker News or GitHub as primary sources for national civic investigations;
* disclose the guest research boundary before the user submits;
* provide deeper signed-in research.

### FTN-FINAL-004 — Hidden keyboard focus

Keyboard focus can enter links inside the visually collapsed Ecosystem menu while `aria-expanded="false"`.

Required repair:

* make collapsed navigation inert;
* remove hidden links from the tab sequence;
* repair the shared header component;
* test every route using keyboard-only navigation.

### FTN-FINAL-005 — 200% zoom failure

The following pages develop horizontal overflow at 200% browser zoom:

* Home;
* FTN Observer;
* FTN Display.

Required repair:

* remove fixed-width or dense-grid constraints causing reflow failure;
* test every major public route at 200% zoom.

### FTN-FINAL-006 — FTN Fire cannot complete its main journey

FTN Fire accepted a detailed beat brief but left Play Draft disabled.

Required completion loop:

**brief → generate draft → play → adjust → export or continue in FTN DAW**

The loop must operate without a paid provider call.

### FTN-FINAL-007 — Contradictory indicator counts

Perplexity found:

* 82 indicators claimed as catalogued in FTN Observer;
* one indicator publicly rendered;
* six indicators in FTN Display;
* 42 referenced by FTN Scenario Workspace.

A client-side readiness filter hides indicators containing placeholder, pending, demonstration or illustrative language.

Required repair:

FTN Statistics becomes the authority for:

* catalogued indicators;
* verified indicators;
* displayable indicators;
* stale indicators;
* unavailable indicators;
* scenario-eligible indicators;
* indicators published in FTN Display.

Every public counter must state exactly what it counts.
