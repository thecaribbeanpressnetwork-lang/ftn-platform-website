# FTN Love — Production Phase 1

## Purpose
Turn FTN Love from a minimal preference form into a useful values-and-intent compatibility foundation without representing nonexistent public profiles, matching, messaging or identity/safety services.

## Operational now
- Build a browser-local compatibility brief covering relationship intent, core values, life priorities, location openness, communication style, preferred pace and a short boundary/context note.
- Carry the current FTN country preference into the saved record as context.
- Save compatibility briefs through the shared FTN Integration Adapter.
- Review recent compatibility briefs stored on the same device.

## Not yet operational
- Public dating profiles.
- Matching or compatibility scoring.
- Recommendations or ranking of people.
- Messaging, calls or media exchange.
- Age, identity or background verification.
- Safety screening, moderation or reporting workflows.
- Precise location sharing.
- Cloud synchronization.
- Payments or subscriptions.

## Product Mnemonic Layer
The FTN Love mnemonic is the **Shared Orbit**: two independent orbits/nodes converging around a shared center. It represents alignment without implying ownership or guaranteed compatibility. Ambiently it establishes product identity; after a successful compatibility-brief save, the orbit and nodes pulse as positive completion feedback. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation.

## Privacy / safety boundary
- Current records stay in the visitor's browser through the shared local adapter.
- The page must not request unnecessary sensitive personal information.
- Precise address, government ID, financial data, sexual history, health data and private contact details are outside this phase.
- A future live social/dating service must add age/identity controls, consent, blocking/reporting, abuse prevention, moderation, retention rules and data deletion before profiles or interpersonal communication are activated.

## Ecosystem seam
Compatibility records are structured FTN data, but no cross-product sharing is activated. Any future ibis.ai or FTN Love matching capability must be permissioned, explainable at an appropriate level, purpose-limited and designed so engagement metrics cannot override user safety or relationship intent.

## Ownership / architecture
- Reuses Product Registry, Workspace Shell, Country Registry, shared storage and Integration Adapter.
- No dating/matching vendor, identity vendor, messaging provider or external credential is introduced.
- Future providers must remain behind shared FTN interfaces rather than becoming the system of record.
- Secrets and provider credentials must never be committed to this repository.
- The deferred **FTN Autonomous Opportunity & Provider Engine** remains separate from this product pass; no provider-routing abstraction is introduced merely for hypothetical future use.

## Merge gate
Browser visual/interactivity review and mobile review are required before merge.
