# FTN Events — Production Phase 1

## Product promise
FTN Events turns a basic event brief into a structured operational planning file covering compliance, venue, production, safety, vendors, marketing, budget, timeline and day-of execution.

## Current production-honest scope
- Rule-based event planning engine with transparent inputs and outputs.
- Concert, Festival, Conference, Wedding, Corporate, Community, Carnival / Fete, Sports, Fundraiser and Other event types.
- Attendance, venue format, budget tier, target date, location, working budget and objective inputs.
- Operational sections for Event Brief; Permits, Legal & Compliance; Venue & Logistics; Production & Programme; Safety, Security & Accessibility; Vendors, Partners & Procurement; Marketing, Ticketing & Communications; Budget & Commercial Control; Planning Timeline; and Day-Of Operations.
- TXT/JSON/print export through the shared Export Framework.
- Local saved-plan history on the user's device.

## Deliberate limitations
- FTN does not claim that generated permit/licensing requirements are authoritative legal advice.
- Requirements must be verified with the responsible authority and venue.
- No ticketing vendor, payment processor, contractor marketplace, permit API, cloud account storage or AI generation is represented as connected until such integrations actually exist.

## Future integration seams
- FTN account/cloud project storage.
- Authority and permit directory by Caribbean jurisdiction.
- Venue/vendor marketplace and request-for-quote workflow.
- Ticketing/registration integration.
- Budget ledger and approval workflow.
- Team assignments, calendar, reminders and run-of-show collaboration.
- ibis.ai assistance layered on top of the structured event record.
