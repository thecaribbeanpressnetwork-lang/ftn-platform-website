# FTN Platform Constitution v1.0

## Preamble

The FTN Platform exists to help communities, institutions and decision-makers understand reality more clearly through trustworthy evidence, transparent methods and long-term stewardship. FTN is not built to persuade, surveil or sensationalize. It is built to improve understanding.

## Article I – Mission

Build trusted intelligence systems that help people understand their communities and country through evidence-based information.

## Article II – Vision

Create a permanent national intelligence platform capable of serving citizens, institutions and future generations while remaining truthful, explainable and transparent.

## Article III – Core Principles

Reality before opinion.
Evidence before assumption.
Understanding before recommendation.
Trust before growth.
Quality before quantity.
Everything is connected.
Communities matter.
Technology should clarify reality.

## Article IV – Product Philosophy

Every FTN product should teach, inform, simplify, encourage curiosity, remain professional, explain itself, and reward exploration.

## Article V – Engineering Philosophy

Build reusable engines before features. Build once, reuse everywhere. Prefer maintainability over cleverness. Accessibility, performance and security are features. Automate verification. Reduce technical debt continuously.

## Article VI – User Experience

Show rather than tell. Reduce clicks. Remove friction. Guide attention intentionally. Every page should answer: What is this? Why should I care? Why should I trust it? What next?

## Article VII – Design Language

Timeless, professional, institutional, warm, restrained and trustworthy. Avoid fashionable trends, clutter and intimidation.

## Article VIII – Data Philosophy

Clearly distinguish Official, Observed, Reported, Derived, Estimated, Modelled and Projected information. Never blur these categories.

## Article IX – Evidence Standard

Every significant claim should expose its source, methodology, freshness and confidence where practical. Transparency is mandatory.

## Article X – Relationships

Clearly distinguish correlation, association, dependency, influence, causation, prediction and recommendation.

## Article XI – Artificial Intelligence

AI assists people, asks better questions, explains reasoning, exposes uncertainty and never replaces accountable human judgement.

## Article XII – Commercial Philosophy

Trust before revenue. Sustainable recurring revenue is preferred. Advertising must never compromise credibility.

## Article XIII – Display Network

Public displays are information experiences first and advertising surfaces second. Information quality is always primary.

## Article XIV – Accessibility

Accessibility is a fundamental requirement, not an enhancement.

## Article XV – Privacy

Collect only what is necessary. Explain why. Respect consent. Operate within Trinidad & Tobago law.

## Article XVI – Platform Architecture

One platform. Many presentation layers. Shared engines power Community Connect, Mission Control, FTN Live, Display Network, APIs, widgets and future products.

## Article XVII – Governance

Major architectural decisions should be documented. Founder decisions are preserved. Technical debt is acknowledged rather than hidden.

## Article XVIII – National Responsibility

Serve facts, not ideology. Improve understanding rather than division.

## Article XIX – The Long View

Design for decades, not hype cycles. Build institutions, not disposable software.

## Article XX – FTN Standard

Every release should leave the platform clearer, faster, safer, more understandable, more trustworthy and more useful than the previous release.
