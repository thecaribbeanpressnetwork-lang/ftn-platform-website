# FTN Commercialization Matrix

FTN / IBIS Quality & UX Closure mission, Priority 4. Built after search reliability was
materially improved (Priority 1 complete, see `FTN_Quality_Pass_2026-09-18.md` and this pass's
own before/after benchmark) -- selling access to a system with a ~46% search failure rate would
have been selling a degraded product; that gate is now cleared.

**Method**: every row below is anchored to a capability independently verified working during
this session or the prior consolidation session (live-tested via the permanent quality benchmark,
the Deno test suite, or direct browser verification) -- not invented. No pricing numbers are
stated as fact; "pricing basis" describes the *logic* a real price would follow, not a quoted
rate. Marginal cost and automation are assessed from the actual architecture (which providers are
called, whether a human step is required), not estimated in the abstract.

## What is explicitly NOT monetized

Public civic functions -- Community Connect, public-interest civic information discovery, the
core ibis chat experience for an ordinary question -- stay free. Monetization sits at the layer
of *institutional scale*, *packaged deliverables*, and *creator convenience tiers on top of an
already-free capability*, never at the layer of "pay to access basic civic information."

## Sequencing

Rows 5 (creator tooling) and 6 (data licensing) are the lowest-risk starting points: both build
directly on capability that already runs today with near-zero incremental marginal cost, and
neither requires a new sales motion beyond what FTN Invest-in's existing partnership conversation
already does. Rows 1-4 and 7 require an institutional sales relationship this document does not
claim FTN currently has -- they are credible destinations, not next week's revenue.
