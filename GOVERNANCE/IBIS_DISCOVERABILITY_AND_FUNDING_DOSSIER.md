# ibis discoverability and funding dossier

**Purpose:** make ibis legible to grant scouts, research programs, public agencies, funders, investors, accelerators, prize committees and strategic partners who encounter the work through search, referrals, public data or the FTN ecosystem.

## One-sentence description

ibis is a Caribbean-first intelligence, intent and execution network that combines regional context, permissioned personal intent, provenance-aware reasoning, opportunity discovery and governed action in a simple cognitive interface called Headspace.

## The problem

Caribbean people, businesses, institutions and creators often have fragmented information, weak discovery, disconnected opportunities and limited access to intelligence infrastructure designed for local reality. Conventional assistants can answer questions, but they do not preserve the user's intent, explain evidence, connect local signals, learn from outcomes or safely move from understanding to action.

## The proposed solution

ibis connects:

- public Caribbean sources and official opportunity feeds;
- private, permissioned intent and founder context;
- a Founder Cognitive Layer that records reasoning and outcomes without claiming consciousness;
- provenance, freshness, confidence and evidence boundaries;
- routing across deterministic engines, models, tools and connected apps;
- permission and cost gates before consequential actions;
- a Headspace interface that assembles the relevant answer, evidence, graph, opportunity or action around the user's objective.

## Why this is fundable

ibis is not only another chatbot. It is a potentially reusable regional intelligence layer with:

1. a differentiated Caribbean data and context position;
2. a permissioned intent graph that can support ethical matching and opportunity discovery;
3. founder-controlled provenance and learning infrastructure;
4. a replaceable provider architecture rather than a single-vendor dependency;
5. potential value to citizens, creators, businesses, governments, researchers and development agencies;
6. a credible path from working deterministic infrastructure to richer model and agent capability as funding becomes available.

## Current evidence

- Founder Cognitive Layer implemented with provenance, integrity digest, pathway learning and retrospective learning.
- Butterfly Engine records expected and unexpected second-order effects.
- Opportunity Intelligence ranks funding and opportunity records against Trinidad and Tobago eligibility, payout access, ownership, fees, effort, probability, scalability and strategic value.
- First-party opportunities adapter is connected through the canonical ibis connection fabric.
- Semantic-memory foundation is applied to the live Supabase project with authenticated, RLS-aware recall function.
- External actions fail closed until a real adapter, authentication and Permission Ledger approval exist.
- Legacy `/ibis-ai/` remains isolated while Headspace develops as a separate page.
