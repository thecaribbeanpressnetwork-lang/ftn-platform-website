# 1. PRIVACY POLICY

## 1.1 Our commitment

FTN exists to help communities, institutions and decision-makers understand the world around them more clearly.

Protecting the privacy, dignity and trust of the people who interact with FTN is fundamental to that mission.

This Privacy Policy explains how the public FTN Platform website at ftnplatform.org currently handles information.

This policy applies only to the public informational website. It does not govern separate FTN applications or services, including Community Connect, Mission Control, Display Network or Media Network applications. Those products will publish their own privacy notices before collecting or processing operational user information.

## 1.2 What the website currently collects

The FTN Platform website does not currently:

* require user registration;
* create user accounts;
* operate a user database;
* collect reports through Community Connect;
* collect GPS coordinates;
* collect photographs or uploaded media;
* collect payment details;
* collect government information;
* collect analytics or advertising identifiers;
* deliberately track users across websites;
* operate a functional contact-submission backend.

The website's Contact form currently performs browser-side validation but does not send, store or transmit the information entered into it.

Visitors should therefore not enter confidential, sensitive, urgent or legally privileged information into the Contact form until a live submission service is formally announced.

## 1.3 Information stored on your device

The website uses browser local storage to remember limited interface preferences.

These preferences may include:

* Presentation Mode or Live Mode selection;
* the position or dismissed state of the Presentation Mode control;
* interface display choices;
* other non-identifying user-experience settings.

These settings remain within the visitor's browser unless the visitor clears browser storage.

The website does not intentionally store names, email addresses, telephone numbers, reports, photographs or other personally identifying information in local storage.

## 1.4 Cookies

The website does not currently set or use cookies.

If cookies, analytics or similar technologies are introduced later, this Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated before or when those technologies are activated.

## 1.5 Third-party services

The website currently requests font files from Google Fonts.

When a visitor's browser requests those files, the third-party provider may receive ordinary technical request information such as the visitor's IP address, browser details and request time as part of normal internet delivery.

FTN does not currently use third-party advertising networks, behavioural analytics services, social-media tracking pixels or embedded payment processors on this website.

The website is hosted through infrastructure that may process ordinary server and security information necessary to deliver and protect the site. Such infrastructure may include Cloudflare Pages and related domain or hosting services configured outside the website repository.

## 1.6 Presentation Mode and Live Mode

The website may operate in Presentation Mode or Live Mode.

Presentation Mode uses representative platform information to demonstrate how FTN products and services are designed to operate.

Live Mode represents the normal production state of the website.

At Version 1.0, both modes may resolve to the same locally supplied datasets because live production engines have not yet been connected.

Presentation Mode does not create a separate copy of the website and does not change the visitor's privacy rights.

## 1.7 Community Connect and other FTN products

The website contains descriptions of separate FTN products that may eventually process information such as:

* community reports;
* photographs;
* location information;
* contact details;
* operational records;
* media uploads;
* government or enterprise information.

Those capabilities are not performed by this website.

Before any separate FTN product begins collecting such information from the public, that product must publish an appropriate privacy policy explaining:

* what it collects;
* why it collects it;
* how it is used;
* where it is stored;
* how long it is retained;
* when it may be shared;
* what rights users have.

## 1.8 Government and law-enforcement requests

FTN does not routinely provide personal information to governments, political organisations, law-enforcement bodies or other third parties.

Where disclosure is lawfully required under the laws of Trinidad and Tobago, FTN may comply with a valid legal obligation or lawful process.

Where legally permitted, FTN will seek to:

* verify the authority and scope of the request;
* disclose only information that is legally required;
* protect user privacy to the greatest extent reasonably possible;
* maintain an appropriate record of the request and response.

At Version 1.0, the public website does not operate a user database or submission system containing report data.

## 1.9 Security

FTN takes reasonable steps to protect the website and its supporting infrastructure.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Visitors should not attempt to:

* gain unauthorised access;
* interfere with the website;
* introduce malicious code;
* bypass security controls;
* misuse any connected service.

## 1.10 Children and young persons

The public informational website is intended for a general audience.

It does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Separate FTN products may establish their own age requirements based on their functions, risks and applicable law.

## 1.11 Your choices

Visitors may:

* browse the website without creating an account;
* clear local storage through their browser settings;
* disable or restrict third-party font requests through browser or network settings;
* leave the website at any time;
* contact FTN through the pathways provided on the Contact page.

## 1.12 Changes to this policy

FTN may update this Privacy Policy when:

* website functionality changes;
* live services are connected;
* data practices change;
* new legal requirements arise;
* separate products are launched.

Material changes will be reflected through a revised effective date and, where appropriate, a notice on the website.

## 1.13 Contact

Questions, concerns or complaints relating to privacy may be submitted through the Privacy or General Enquiries pathway on the FTN Contact page.

# 4. DATA RETENTION POLICY

## 4.1 Purpose

This policy explains how the public FTN Platform website currently retains information.

It applies only to ftnplatform.org and not to separate FTN applications or enterprise systems.

## 4.2 Current website data

The Version 1.0 website does not operate:

* a user-account database;
* a contact-submission database;
* a civic-report database;
* a media-upload database;
* a payment database;
* an analytics database.

The website therefore does not currently retain user-submitted personal information through its own application code.

## 4.3 Local device preferences

Interface preferences stored through local storage remain on the visitor's device until:

* the visitor clears them;
* the browser removes them;
* the website changes or retires the relevant storage key.

FTN does not currently receive or centrally retain those preferences.

## 4.4 Hosting and infrastructure records

The website's hosting, domain, security or delivery providers may retain ordinary technical records such as:

* IP addresses;
* request timestamps;
* browser or device information;
* security events;
* error logs;
* network request details.

Those records are controlled through the relevant infrastructure accounts and provider arrangements rather than the website's client-side application code.

Where FTN can configure retention, it should retain such records only for legitimate security, operational, legal or diagnostic purposes.

## 4.5 Third-party resources

Third-party providers such as font or hosting providers may retain request information according to their own policies.

FTN does not control every aspect of third-party retention.

## 4.6 Retention principles

When FTN begins centrally retaining information, it will apply the following principles:

* collect only what is reasonably necessary;
* define the reason for retention;
* restrict access;
* protect information appropriately;
* retain information only for legitimate operational, historical, contractual or legal purposes;
* review retention periods;
* securely delete or anonymise information when continued retention is no longer justified;
* preserve information where required by valid legal process.

## 4.7 Separate FTN products

Community Connect and other operational FTN products will require separate retention schedules before processing public submissions.

Those schedules must address, where applicable:

* reports;
* photographs;
* GPS information;
* contact details;
* moderation records;
* audit trails;
* backups;
* account information;
* government or enterprise records;
* legal preservation requirements.

This website policy must not be interpreted as the retention policy for those separate products.

## 4.8 Legal preservation

FTN may preserve relevant information where reasonably necessary to:

* comply with a lawful obligation;
* respond to litigation or anticipated litigation;
* investigate security incidents;
* enforce contractual or legal rights;
* protect users, the public or the platform.

## 4.9 Review

This Data Retention Policy must be reviewed whenever the website begins collecting or centrally storing new categories of information.

# 5. PLATFORM TRANSPARENCY STATEMENT

*(Not yet published as standalone site content — see the integration note at the top of this file.)*

FTN is independent and non-partisan.

It does not exist to support a political party, candidate or administration.

FTN exists to improve public understanding, strengthen accountability, support better decisions and help communities see the larger patterns behind individual experiences.

FTN recognises that:

* information can be incomplete;
* data can be misunderstood;
* automated systems can be wrong;
* demonstration information is not the same as verified production information;
* technology should support human judgment rather than replace it.

Where automated tools are introduced, their purpose will be to organise information, identify patterns and support understanding. Responsibility for consequential decisions remains with appropriately authorised human users.

FTN will not present Presentation Mode information as official government data.

FTN will not claim that separate products are operational when they remain conceptual or under development.

FTN will update its policies as the platform moves from presentation systems to live production services.

# 6. COMMUNITY AND PUBLIC-INFORMATION PRINCIPLES

*(Not yet published as standalone site content — see the integration note at the top of this file.)*

These principles will govern future FTN public-participation products:

* Country over party.
* Evidence over rumour.
* Understanding over outrage.
* Constructive participation over abuse.
* Accountability without harassment.
* Privacy without secrecy.
* Transparency without recklessness.
* Technology in service of people.
* No one should be targeted because of political affiliation, ethnicity, religion, community, disability, gender or other protected status.
* Reports and public contributions must not be used for threats, defamation, discrimination, false accusations or political campaigning.
* FTN should help people and institutions understand problems, not inflame them.

Separate Community Connect terms and reporting standards must be completed before the application's public-store release.
