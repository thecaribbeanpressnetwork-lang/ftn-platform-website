About FTN Index

Frequently asked questions

Caribbean information is spread across websites, social platforms, maps, directories, government sources and other public channels. FTN Index brings useful public facts together into structured Caribbean records, while keeping track of where information came from and when it was last confirmed.

What is FTN Index?

FTN Index is a Caribbean information project from FTN Platform. It organizes public information about businesses and organizations into structured records that are easier for people, search systems and emerging AI services to understand and use.

Why is my business in FTN Index?

While building the Index, FTN may identify publicly available information associated with Caribbean businesses and organizations. The purpose is to make regional information clearer, more consistent and easier to verify.

Where did FTN get my information?

FTN Index uses publicly available sources. Records are designed to preserve source provenance so FTN can distinguish information it discovered from information later confirmed or corrected by the business itself.

What information can FTN Index contain?

Depending on the type of business and what is publicly available, a record may include the business name, category, description, location, address, phone, public email, website, opening hours, services, booking or enquiry links and other category-specific information. Social identities can be recorded separately for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, YouTube, LinkedIn and other relevant channels.

What does [Not found] mean?

It means FTN did not locate that field in the public sources checked for the record. It does not mean the information or account does not exist. Businesses can add or correct missing information when reviewing their record.

Why does FTN show social-media handles separately?

A social account is part of a business's public digital identity. Showing each platform separately helps identify missing, outdated or incorrectly associated accounts instead of treating every social presence as one generic field.

How do I correct my business information?

If FTN sends your business a verification invitation, the button in that message opens the FTN Index record prepared for that business. The information is pre-filled so you can correct what is wrong, add what is missing and confirm the result. You do not need to purchase a listing to correct factual information.

Can I reply to the FTN email instead?

Yes. A business can reply to an FTN Index message or contact FTN about a correction. The structured review page is the fastest way to update several fields at once, while written correspondence can also provide evidence for a proposed correction.

Does it cost anything?

No. Businesses are not charged to review, correct or confirm factual information about themselves in FTN Index.

What does the FTN confirmation mark mean?

It means the indexed public information was recently confirmed through the FTN Index process. It is a record of confirmation and freshness, not an endorsement, safety certification, quality guarantee or recommendation.

What is verification freshness?

Information changes over time. FTN records when information was confirmed so users and systems can distinguish recently confirmed information from information that has aged since its last confirmation.

Does not responding to FTN mean something is wrong with my business?

No. FTN evaluates information based on evidence, sources, consistency and freshness. Communication behavior is not evidence that a business is closed or untrustworthy.

Does FTN claim that all indexed information is correct?

No. FTN Index distinguishes information found in public sources from information confirmed by the business and from information supported by other evidence. That distinction is central to the Index.

Is FTN affiliated with Google or another search or AI company?

No affiliation should be inferred. FTN Index is an FTN Platform project. Its goal is to create better structured Caribbean information that can be useful to people and, where appropriate, interoperable with search, mapping, data and AI systems.

Why is FTN doing this?

Caribbean information is often fragmented across many public sources and represented inconsistently. FTN is building a Caribbean-specific normalization and confirmation layer so regional entities can be represented with clearer structure, provenance and freshness.

Can a business ask to be added?

Yes. Businesses and members of the public can contact FTN about a business or organization that should be considered for the Index. FTN can then research the entity through the normal sourcing, matching and quality process.

Who is FTN?

FTN Platform is a Caribbean digital ecosystem building connected information, civic, media and intelligence infrastructure. FTN Index is the structured knowledge layer for Caribbean entities and their public information.
