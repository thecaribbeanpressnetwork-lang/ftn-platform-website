Case 01 · Ecosystem brand architecture

Founder · Executive Architect · Brand Direction

FTN is one institution expressed through focused products. My role is to preserve a recognizable parent identity while ensuring each product can signal its own purpose. Brand decisions are evaluated across web, mobile, navigation, hero composition, product naming, imagery, trust/provenance surfaces and cross-product consistency. The objective is not visual sameness; it is institutional coherence.

Brand systems at physical scale

Earlier in my career I was responsible for implementing established brand environments at national scale. I did not design these companies’ identities; my role was to make the brand survive thousands of real-world touchpoints accurately and consistently.
3,624Tim Hortons locations in a national LCD menu-board rollout.
700Shoppers Drug Mart stores in a manufactured/installed/merchandised fixture rollout.
5Big Box RONA developments managed, with up to CDN $2M in fixtures/millwork/equipment per store.
7Party City locations developed from concept to grand opening.

How I work

The design role I perform best is systems-oriented: define the logic, make it repeatable, validate the edge cases and protect coherence as the number of touchpoints grows.
Identity before decoration

Every visual decision should reinforce what the brand is, not merely make a screen look busy.

Consistency without sameness

Related products should feel connected while preserving enough distinction to remain memorable.

Human QA is the gate

Tools—including AI—can accelerate production. Final quality, authenticity and brand judgment remain human decisions.

Design for the real medium

Brand DNA has to survive mobile, desktop, presentations, social assets, print and physical execution.

Caribbean specificity matters

Regional identity should come from real people, places, culture and context—not generic tropical stereotypes.

Build rules that scale

A strong brand system reduces future inconsistency and makes quality easier to maintain as teams and products expand.
