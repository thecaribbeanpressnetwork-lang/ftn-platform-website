Where Did Casey Go?

Where Did Casey Go?: Volume 1 — Dealing with the Loss of a Pet is a 32-page children's book published under my pen name, MR Zen.

The story is designed to help parents begin difficult conversations with children about grief and loss. It uses familiar imagery and characters who express grief, then moves toward a gentler idea of transition, memory and an emotionally safe place for a child to land.
