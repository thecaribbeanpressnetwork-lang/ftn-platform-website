The career arc matters

The range is the point. Each phase added a different operating lens. Together they create pattern recognition that a narrow career path rarely produces.

1990s

Frontline → management

Service, inventory, tax surveillance, hospitality and retail operations. Learned customers, process, accountability and the reality beneath management theory.

Late 1990s–2000s

Stores → systems

Party City store development, Danier management, BroFort field execution, RONA national fixtures/equipment, Money Mart district management and M&M construction.

2008 onward

Risk & judgment

RCMP policing followed by investigations and loss prevention added evidence, ambiguity, risk and decision-making under pressure.

2012–2019

National projects → ownership

Managed national retail programs and business development, then founded T.A.G. Retail Installation, evolved it into The Shopfitters.ca and sold the Canadian business before returning to Trinidad & Tobago.

2019–now

Entrepreneurship → digital infrastructure

Trinidad ventures, media, consulting, digital commerce and now FTN: a shift from operating individual businesses toward building reusable Caribbean-owned systems.
