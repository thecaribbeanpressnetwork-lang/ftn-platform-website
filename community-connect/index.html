Community Connect

Report. Connect. Improve.

Your voice. Your community. Your future. Community Connect is the free, citizen-facing side of FTN Platform — a direct way to report issues, follow their progress, and see what's happening across the places you live.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Community Connect.

How does it work?

You capture a photo of the issue, describe it, pin its location, and submit — your report is then tracked from submission through to resolution, and you can follow its status at any time.

Is my data safe?

Report data is encrypted in transit, and you're never required to share more personal information than a report needs. See the Privacy section above for details, and our Privacy Policy for the full picture once it's published.

Do I need an account?

Many issues can be reported without an account. Creating a free account lets you track your reports over time and follow their progress.
