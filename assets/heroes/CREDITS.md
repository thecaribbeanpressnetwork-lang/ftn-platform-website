# Hero image credits

Every hero image in this folder is either FTN-owned production photography or extracted from the
approved AEB asset library, **except the one listed below**, which is a real third-party photograph
used under its stated Creative Commons license. Record any future externally-sourced asset here in
the same format before adding it to `/assets/heroes/`.

## `ftn-govern-red-house.webp`

- **Subject:** The Red House, Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago (seat of Parliament)
- **Source file:** `File:POS Redhouse.JPG`, Wikimedia Commons
- **Source URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:POS_Redhouse.JPG
- **Photographer:** Anthony Mendenhall (Wikimedia user "Random 00021")
- **License:** CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-23
- **Modifications:** re-encoded from the original JPEG to WebP (quality 82) for web delivery; no
  crop, no resize, no upscale — shipped at the source's native 1544×1024, honestly below the
  1920×1080 target rather than artificially upscaled.
- **ShareAlike notice:** per CC BY-SA, this WebP derivative is licensed under the same
  CC BY-SA 3.0 terms as the original.
- Four candidates from the same Wikimedia Commons category
  (`Category:The_Red_House_(Trinidad_and_Tobago)`) were compared before this one was selected —
  this file had the cleanest composition (centered dome, real sky headroom for headline
  typography, no visible scaffolding/renovation, minimal power-line clutter) of the four real,
  non-AI photographs found. Two higher-resolution candidates existed but were visibly obstructed
  (renovation scaffolding, a traffic light, parked cars in the frame) and were not used.
