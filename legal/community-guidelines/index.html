Community Guidelines

Participate with evidence, consent and respect for the people affected.

Do

  • Submit information you believe is accurate and have authority to share.
  • Use source links and distinguish fact, opinion and personal experience.
  • Respect privacy, safety, copyright and lawful restrictions.
  • Use block/report/correction tools when available.

Do not

  • Threaten, harass, dox, impersonate or encourage violence.
  • Upload intimate, health, identity or precise-location information without a lawful, explicit purpose and consent.
  • Spam, scrape, enumerate users or coordinate deceptive engagement.
  • Present rumours, generated content or edited media as verified fact.
FTN may reject, limit, correct, unpublish, preserve as evidence or refer content where necessary for safety, law or platform integrity. A submission receipt is not publication or endorsement. Appeals and correction requests should identify the affected URL or transaction ID through FTN Contact.
