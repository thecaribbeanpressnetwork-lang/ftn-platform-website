Cookie Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026 · Operator: RealityArtTV Media, operating the FTN Platform · Governing jurisdiction: Republic of Trinidad and Tobago

Current Position Local Browser Storage Third-Party Resources Browser Controls Future Use Updates Contact

Current Position

The FTN Platform website does not currently set cookies.

The website does not currently use:

  • behavioural advertising cookies;
  • cross-site tracking cookies;
  • analytics cookies;
  • social-media tracking pixels;
  • user-authentication cookies;
  • payment cookies.

Local Browser Storage

The website uses local browser storage to remember limited interface preferences.

These settings may include:

  • Presentation Mode or Live Mode;
  • the floating Presentation Mode control position;
  • whether the control was dismissed during a page view;
  • other interface preferences.

This information is stored on the visitor’s device and is not intentionally transmitted by the website to FTN.

Third-Party Resources

The website currently requests font resources from Google Fonts.

That request is made by the visitor’s browser to the third-party provider and may involve ordinary technical request information.

The third party’s own terms and privacy practices govern its processing.

Browser Controls

Visitors can clear local storage, restrict third-party resources or change privacy settings through their browser.

Clearing local storage may reset interface preferences.

Future Use

FTN may introduce cookies or similar technologies in the future for legitimate purposes such as:

  • security;
  • authentication;
  • accessibility;
  • consent management;
  • analytics;
  • service delivery.

Where required, FTN will provide appropriate notice and choice before or when such technologies are introduced.

Updates

This policy will be updated when the website’s use of cookies, analytics or browser storage changes.

Contact

Questions about this Cookie Policy may be submitted through the General Enquiries pathway on the FTN Contact page.
