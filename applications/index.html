Long-Term Initiatives

Permanent areas of institutional focus.

FTN Display Network

A long-term initiative to connect businesses, institutions, and communities through a trusted Caribbean-wide public information display network — built on the same real-time indicator infrastructure as FTN Live.

FTN Media Network

The Caribbean's long-term public media infrastructure, bringing together journalism, broadcasting, podcasts, investigative reporting, and public-interest storytelling under one trusted institutional platform. Artist & Creative Services enquiries are welcomed today.
